# Physical Divisions of India

India has **6 major physiographic divisions**:

1. Himalayan Mountains
2. Northern Plains
3. Peninsular Plateau
4. Indian Desert
5. Coastal Plains
6. Islands

---

# Peninsular Plateau

Oldest physical division.

- Central Highlands
- Deccan Plateau

# Indian Desert

**Thar Desert**

Location:

Rajasthan

Characteristics:

- Sandy dunes.
- Low rainfall.
- Luni River (largest river of the Thar).

- Coral Islands

- Volcanic Origin
- Barren Island

Black:
- Cotton

- Wheat & Rice

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Mount Everest is the highest peak in India.

✅ **Mount Everest** is on the **Nepal–China border**.
**Kanchenjunga** is the highest peak associated with India.

---

❌ Narmada flows into the Bay of Bengal.

✅ **Narmada** and **Tapi** flow westwards into the **Arabian Sea**.

---

❌ Black soil is best suited for rice cultivation.

✅ **Black soil** is ideal for **cotton** due to its moisture-retaining capacity.

---

❌ Lakshadweep is of volcanic origin.

✅ **Lakshadweep** consists of **coral atolls**.
**Andaman & Nicobar** includes **Barren Island**, India's only active volcano.

---

❌ Chilika is the largest freshwater lake in India.

✅ **Chilika** is the **largest brackish water lagoon** in India.
**Wular** is one of India's largest freshwater lakes.
