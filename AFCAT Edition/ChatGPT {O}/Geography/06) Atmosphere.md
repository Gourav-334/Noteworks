# Chapter 6: Atmosphere

> **Purpose:** Study the composition, structure, temperature, pressure, winds, humidity, clouds and rainfall of the Earth's atmosphere.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT commonly asks about **layers of the atmosphere, ozone layer, trade winds, jet streams, humidity, cloud types and rainfall**.

---

# Background

The atmosphere is the **blanket of gases** surrounding the Earth.

Functions:

- Supports life.
- Regulates temperature.
- Protects Earth from harmful radiation.
- Burns up most meteoroids before they reach Earth.

---

# Composition of the Atmosphere

| Gas | Approx. Percentage |
|------|-------------------:|
| Nitrogen | 78% |
| Oxygen | 21% |
| Argon | 0.93% |
| Carbon Dioxide | ~0.04% |
| Other Gases | Trace Amounts |

---

# Importance of Atmospheric Gases

## Nitrogen

- Plant growth.
- Protein formation.

---

## Oxygen

- Respiration.
- Combustion.

---

## Carbon Dioxide

- Photosynthesis.
- Greenhouse Effect.

---

## Ozone

Absorbs:

**Ultraviolet (UV) Rays**

---

# Layers of the Atmosphere

| Layer | Approx. Height | Important Feature |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Troposphere | 0–8 km (Poles), 0–18 km (Equator) | Weather Phenomena |
| Stratosphere | ~18–50 km | Ozone Layer |
| Mesosphere | ~50–80 km | Meteors Burn Up |
| Thermosphere | ~80–700 km | Ionosphere & Auroras |
| Exosphere | Above ~700 km | Merges into Space |

---

# Troposphere

Lowest layer.

Contains:

About **75% of atmospheric mass**.

Weather occurs here.

Temperature:

Decreases with height.

Average Lapse Rate:

**6.5°C per 1000 m**

---

# Stratosphere

Contains:

**Ozone Layer**

Commercial aircraft often cruise in the lower stratosphere because:

- Stable air.
- Less turbulence.

Temperature:

Increases with height due to ozone absorbing UV radiation.

---

# Mesosphere

Coldest atmospheric layer.

Most meteors burn up here due to friction.

---

# Thermosphere

Contains:

**Ionosphere**

Importance:

- Radio Communication.
- Aurora Borealis.
- Aurora Australis.

Temperature:

Increases rapidly with height.

---

# Exosphere

Outermost layer.

Contains:

Very thin gases.

Artificial satellites orbit through the upper thermosphere and exosphere.

---

# Atmospheric Pressure

Pressure exerted by the weight of the atmosphere.

Measured by:

**Barometer**

Unit:

Millibar (mb) or hectopascal (hPa)

Standard Sea-Level Pressure:

**1013.25 mb**

---

# Pressure Belts

Major Pressure Belts:

- Equatorial Low Pressure
- Subtropical High Pressure
- Subpolar Low Pressure
- Polar High Pressure

---

# Winds

Wind:

Horizontal movement of air from:

**High Pressure → Low Pressure**

## Permanent (Planetary) Winds

## Seasonal Winds

---

# Trade Winds

Blow:

From:

Subtropical High Pressure

Towards:

Equatorial Low Pressure.

Direction:

- Northern Hemisphere → Northeast Trade Winds
- Southern Hemisphere → Southeast Trade Winds

---

# Westerlies

Blow between:

30°–60° Latitude.

Direction:

West → East.

---

# Polar Easterlies

Blow from:

Polar High Pressure.

Direction:

East → West.

---

# Jet Streams

Fast-moving narrow air currents in the upper troposphere.

Importance:

- Influence weather.
- Affect aviation.
- Help determine monsoon patterns.

---

# Humidity

Amount of water vapour in the atmosphere.

**Hygrometer**

---

# Types of Humidity

- Absolute Humidity
- Relative Humidity
- Specific Humidity

> **Relative Humidity** is the most commonly asked in exams.

# Types of Rainfall

## Convectional Rainfall

Caused by:

Heating of land.

Common in:

Equatorial Regions.

---

## Orographic (Relief) Rainfall

Caused by:

Mountains obstructing moist winds.

Example:

Western Ghats.

---

## Cyclonic Rainfall

Caused by:

Meeting of different air masses or cyclonic systems.

---

# Temperature Inversion

Normally:

Temperature decreases with height.

During inversion:

Temperature increases with height near the surface.

Effects:

- Fog
- Smog
- Air Pollution Trapping

---

# Greenhouse Effect

Main Greenhouse Gases:

- Carbon Dioxide
- Methane
- Water Vapour
- Nitrous Oxide

Importance:

Keeps Earth warm.

Excessive greenhouse gases lead to:

**Global Warming**

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Troposphere ≠ Stratosphere

Troposphere:
- Weather

Stratosphere:
- Ozone Layer

---

### Mesosphere ≠ Thermosphere

Mesosphere:
- Meteors Burn

Thermosphere:
- Ionosphere
- Auroras

---

### Barometer ≠ Hygrometer

Barometer:
- Pressure

Hygrometer:
- Humidity

---

### Convectional ≠ Orographic Rainfall

Convectional:
- Heating

Orographic:
- Mountains

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The ozone layer is found in the Troposphere.

✅ The **ozone layer is located in the Stratosphere**.

---

❌ Meteors burn up in the Thermosphere.

✅ Most meteors burn up in the **Mesosphere**.

---

❌ Atmospheric pressure is measured by a Hygrometer.

✅ A **Barometer** measures atmospheric pressure.
A **Hygrometer** measures humidity.

---

❌ Weather occurs in the Stratosphere.

✅ Almost all **weather phenomena occur in the Troposphere**.

---

❌ Greenhouse gases are always harmful.

✅ The **natural greenhouse effect is essential for life**. Excess greenhouse gases enhance warming and contribute to **climate change**.
