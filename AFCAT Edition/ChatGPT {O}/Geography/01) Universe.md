# Big Bang Theory

Proposed that:

- The Universe began from an extremely hot and dense state.
- It has been expanding ever since.

> **The Big Bang describes the beginning of the observable Universe—not an explosion in empty space, but the expansion of space itself.**

# Important Planet Facts

## Mercury

- Closest planet to the Sun.
- Smallest planet.
- No natural satellite.
- No atmosphere of significance.

---

## Venus

- Hottest planet.
- Rotates **east to west** (retrograde rotation).
- Slowest rotation.
- Called:

**Morning Star** and **Evening Star**.

No natural satellite.

---

## Earth

- Only known planet supporting life.
- Known as:

**Blue Planet**

One natural satellite:

**Moon**

---

## Mars

Known as:

**Red Planet**

Reason:

Iron oxide (rust) on its surface.

Has two moons:

- Phobos
- Deimos

---

## Jupiter

- Largest planet.
- Fastest rotation.
- Great Red Spot (giant storm).
- Largest number of known moons *(current counts change as new discoveries are confirmed).*

---

## Saturn

Known for:

- Prominent ring system.

Least dense planet.

Could float in water *(theoretically, if a large enough ocean existed).*

---

## Uranus

- Rotates on its side.
- Retrograde rotation.
- Ice Giant.

---

## Neptune

- Farthest planet from the Sun.
- Strongest planetary winds.

# Moon

Earth's only natural satellite.

Characteristics:

- No atmosphere of significance.
- No liquid water on the surface.
- Gravity ≈ one-sixth of Earth's.

Always shows the same face to Earth because of:

**Synchronous Rotation**

---

# Asteroids

Rocky bodies orbiting the Sun.

Most are found in:

**Asteroid Belt**

Located between:

- Mars

# Comets

Made mainly of:

- Ice
- Dust
- Frozen gases

- Coma
- Tail

when approaching the Sun.

> **The comet's tail always points away from the Sun due to solar radiation and the solar wind.**

- Reaches Earth

### Mercury ≠ Venus

- Closest to Sun

- Hottest Planet

- Blue Planet

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Mercury is the hottest planet.

✅ **Venus** is the hottest planet due to its **dense carbon dioxide atmosphere** and **runaway greenhouse effect**.

---

❌ Pluto is the ninth planet.

✅ Since **2006**, **Pluto is classified as a dwarf planet**.

---

❌ A meteorite burns up completely in the atmosphere.

✅ A **meteor** is the streak of light in the atmosphere.
A **meteorite** is the portion that reaches Earth's surface.

---

❌ The comet's tail points in the direction of its motion.

✅ A comet's **tail always points away from the Sun**, regardless of its direction of travel.

---

❌ The Moon has no gravity.

✅ The Moon **does have gravity**, but it is **about one-sixth** of Earth's gravity.
