# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Nile ≠ Amazon

Nile:
- Longest River

Amazon:
- Largest Water Discharge

---

### Caspian Sea ≠ Lake Superior

Caspian:
- Largest Inland Water Body

Lake Superior:
- Largest Freshwater Lake (Surface Area)

---

### Sahara ≠ Antarctica

Sahara:
- Largest Hot Desert

Antarctica:
- Largest Desert Overall

---

### Suez ≠ Panama Canal

Suez:
- Mediterranean ↔ Red Sea

Panama:
- Atlantic ↔ Pacific

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Amazon is the longest river in the world.

✅ **Nile** is generally accepted as the **longest river** for AFCAT.
**Amazon** has the **largest water discharge**.

---

❌ Australia is the smallest country.

✅ Australia is the **smallest continent**.
(Vatican City is the world's smallest sovereign country.)

---

❌ The Panama Canal connects the Mediterranean and Red Sea.

✅ The **Panama Canal** connects the **Atlantic Ocean** and the **Pacific Ocean**.
The **Suez Canal** connects the **Mediterranean Sea** and the **Red Sea**.

---

❌ South Africa has only one capital.

✅ South Africa has **three capitals**:
- Pretoria (Executive)
- Cape Town (Legislative)
- Bloemfontein (Judicial)

---

❌ The Caspian Sea is a freshwater lake.

✅ The **Caspian Sea** is the **world's largest inland water body** and is **saline**, not freshwater.
