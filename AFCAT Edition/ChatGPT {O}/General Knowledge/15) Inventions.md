# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Charles Babbage invented the World Wide Web.

✅ **Charles Babbage** is known as the **Father of the Computer**.
The **World Wide Web** was invented by **Tim Berners-Lee**.

---

❌ Alexander Fleming discovered the Smallpox vaccine.

✅ **Alexander Fleming** discovered **Penicillin**.
The **Smallpox vaccine** was developed by **Edward Jenner**.

---

❌ Marconi invented the telephone.

✅ **Alexander Graham Bell** invented the **telephone**.
**Guglielmo Marconi** is associated with the **radio**.

---

❌ James Watt invented the aeroplane.

✅ **James Watt** is associated with the **steam engine**.
The **Wright Brothers** made the first successful powered airplane flight.

---

❌ Thomas Edison invented the computer.

✅ **Thomas Edison** is associated with the **practical incandescent electric bulb**.
**Charles Babbage** is known as the **Father of the Computer**.
