# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Bharat Ratna ≠ Param Vir Chakra

Bharat Ratna:
- Civilian

Param Vir Chakra:
- Military (War)

---

### Param Vir Chakra ≠ Ashoka Chakra

Param Vir Chakra:
- Wartime Gallantry

Ashoka Chakra:
- Peacetime Gallantry

---

### Arjuna Award ≠ Dronacharya Award

Arjuna:
- Player

Dronacharya:
- Coach

---

### Nobel Prize ≠ Booker Prize

Nobel:
- Multiple Fields

Booker:
- Fiction Literature

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Padma Vibhushan is India's highest civilian award.

✅ **Bharat Ratna** is India's **highest civilian award**.

---

❌ Ashoka Chakra is awarded for wartime bravery.

✅ **Param Vir Chakra** is awarded for **wartime gallantry**.
**Ashoka Chakra** is awarded for **peacetime gallantry**.

---

❌ C. V. Raman was the first Indian Nobel laureate.

✅ **Rabindranath Tagore** was the **first Indian Nobel laureate (1913)**.
**C. V. Raman** was the **first Indian Nobel laureate in Science (1930)**.

---

❌ Arjuna Award is given to coaches.

✅ **Arjuna Award** is presented to **sportspersons**.
**Dronacharya Award** is presented to **coaches**.

---

❌ Bhanu Athaiya won the Booker Prize.

✅ **Bhanu Athaiya** was the **first Indian Oscar winner**.
The **Booker Prize** is an international literary award.
