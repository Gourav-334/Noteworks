# Chapter 11: Temples

> **Purpose:** Study India's famous temples, Jyotirlingas, Shakti Peethas and important temple architecture.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT usually asks **Temple ↔ State**, **Temple ↔ Deity**, **UNESCO status**, and occasionally **architectural style**.

---

# Background

Indian temples are important for:

- Religion
- Architecture
- History
- Culture

Major Temple Architecture Styles:

- Nagara (North India)
- Dravida (South India)
- Vesara (Mixed Style)

---

# Temple Architecture

## Nagara Style

Region:

North India

Features:

- Curved Shikhara
- Square Plan
- No large gateway (Gopuram)

Examples:

- Khajuraho Temples
- Konark Sun Temple

---

## Dravida Style

Region:

South India

Features:

- Pyramid-shaped Vimana
- Massive Gopurams
- Temple Tanks

Examples:

- Brihadeeswara Temple
- Meenakshi Temple

---

## Vesara Style

Region:

Deccan

Features:

Combination of:

- Nagara
- Dravida

Examples:

- Pattadakal Temples
- Hoysala Temples

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Konark Sun Temple is located in Gujarat.

✅ **Konark Sun Temple** is located in **Odisha** and is dedicated to the **Sun God (Surya)**.

---

❌ Kedarnath is dedicated to Lord Vishnu.

✅ **Kedarnath** is dedicated to **Lord Shiva**.
**Badrinath** is dedicated to **Lord Vishnu**.

---

❌ Golden Temple is a Hindu temple.

✅ The **Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib)** is the **holiest shrine of Sikhism**, located in **Amritsar, Punjab**.

---

❌ Char Dham and Chota Char Dham are the same pilgrimage circuit.

✅ **Char Dham** includes **Badrinath, Dwarka, Jagannath Puri and Rameshwaram**.
**Chota Char Dham** consists of **Badrinath, Kedarnath, Gangotri and Yamunotri**, all in Uttarakhand.

---

❌ All UNESCO temple sites are located in South India.

✅ UNESCO-listed temples are spread across multiple states, including **Odisha (Konark)**, **Madhya Pradesh (Khajuraho)**, **Karnataka (Hampi and Pattadakal)** and **Tamil Nadu (Brihadeeswara)**.
