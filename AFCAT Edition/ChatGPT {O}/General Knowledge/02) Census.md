# Background

The **Census of India** is the official survey of the country's population.

Conducted by:

**Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner, India**

Under:

**Ministry of Home Affairs**

Conducted:

**Every 10 years (Decennial Census)**

---

# Census

Meaning:

Official collection of information regarding:

- Population
- Housing
- Literacy
- Occupation
- Demographic characteristics

# Population

Meaning:

Total number of people living in a country.

---

# Population Growth

Meaning:

Increase in population over time.

Affected by:

- Birth Rate
- Death Rate
- Migration

---

# Population Density

Meaning:

Number of people living per square kilometre.

Formula:

**Population ÷ Area**

Unit:

**Persons/km²**

---

# Literacy Rate

Meaning:

Percentage of persons aged **7 years and above**

who can:

Read and write with understanding.

---

# Sex Ratio

Meaning:

Number of females

per

1000 males.

---

# Child Sex Ratio

Meaning:

Number of girls (0–6 years)

per

1000 boys.

---

# Urban Population

Population living in:

Cities and towns.

---

# Rural Population

Population living in:

Villages.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Population ≠ Population Density

Population:
- Total Number of People

Population Density:
- People per km²

---

### Literacy ≠ Education Level

Literacy:
- Ability to Read & Write

Education Level:
- Higher Academic Qualification

---

### Sex Ratio ≠ Child Sex Ratio

Sex Ratio:
- Total Females per 1000 Males

Child Sex Ratio:
- Girls (0–6 years) per 1000 Boys

---

### Rajasthan ≠ Uttar Pradesh

Rajasthan:
- Largest by Area

Uttar Pradesh:
- Largest by Population

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Census is conducted every five years.

✅ The **Census of India** is conducted **every 10 years**.

---

❌ Rajasthan has the highest population.

✅ **Rajasthan** is the **largest state by area**.
**Uttar Pradesh** has the **largest population**.

---

❌ Literacy Rate measures the number of school graduates.

✅ **Literacy Rate** measures the percentage of people **aged 7 years and above who can read and write with understanding**.

---

❌ Population Density means total population.

✅ **Population Density** is the **number of persons living per square kilometre**.

---

❌ Kerala has the highest population density among states.

✅ **Kerala** has the **highest literacy rate**.
**Bihar** has the **highest population density among states** (2011 Census).
