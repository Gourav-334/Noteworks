# Chapter 3: Important Days

> **Purpose:** Study important National and International Days, their dates, significance and major observances.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT usually asks **date-based one-liners**. Focus on the **most frequently observed days**, not every UN observance.

---

# Background

Important Days are observed to:

- Raise awareness.
- Commemorate historical events.
- Promote global cooperation.
- Highlight social, environmental and health issues.

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Army Day is celebrated on 26 January.

✅ **Army Day** is celebrated on **15 January**.
**26 January** is **Republic Day**.

---

❌ International Day of Yoga is observed on 5 June.

✅ **International Day of Yoga** is observed on **21 June**.
**5 June** is **World Environment Day**.

---

❌ Navy Day is celebrated on 8 October.

✅ **Navy Day** is celebrated on **4 December**.
**Air Force Day** is celebrated on **8 October**.

---

❌ Gandhi Jayanti and Martyrs' Day are observed on the same date.

✅ **Gandhi Jayanti** is observed on **2 October**.
**Martyrs' Day** is observed on **30 January**.

---

❌ World Health Day is celebrated on 24 October.

✅ **World Health Day** is observed on **7 April**.
**24 October** is **United Nations Day**.
