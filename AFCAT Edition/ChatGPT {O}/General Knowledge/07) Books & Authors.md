# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Gitanjali ≠ Godaan

Gitanjali:
- Rabindranath Tagore

Godaan:
- Munshi Premchand

---

### Discovery of India ≠ India Wins Freedom

Discovery of India:
- Jawaharlal Nehru

India Wins Freedom:
- Maulana Abul Kalam Azad

---

### Akbarnama ≠ Ain-i-Akbari

Both written by:

**Abul Fazl**

Akbarnama:
- History of Akbar's reign

Ain-i-Akbari:
- Administration of Akbar's Empire

---

### Wings of Fire ≠ Ignited Minds

Both by:

A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Wings of Fire:
- Autobiography

Ignited Minds:
- Vision for India's youth

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Gitanjali was written by Munshi Premchand.

✅ **Gitanjali** was written by **Rabindranath Tagore**.
**Godaan** was written by **Munshi Premchand**.

---

❌ Discovery of India was written by Mahatma Gandhi.

✅ **Discovery of India** was written by **Jawaharlal Nehru**.
**Hind Swaraj** was written by **Mahatma Gandhi**.

---

❌ Akbarnama was written by Babur.

✅ **Akbarnama** was written by **Abul Fazl**.
**Baburnama** was written by **Babur** himself.

---

❌ Wings of Fire is a science textbook.

✅ **Wings of Fire** is the **autobiography of Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam**, co-authored with **Arun Tiwari**.

---

❌ Ramcharitmanas was written by Valmiki.

✅ **Ramcharitmanas** was written by **Tulsidas**.
**Ramayana** was written by **Valmiki**.
