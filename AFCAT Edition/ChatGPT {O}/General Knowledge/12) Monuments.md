# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Taj Mahal was built by Akbar.

✅ **Taj Mahal** was built by **Shah Jahan** in memory of **Mumtaz Mahal**.

---

❌ Qutub Minar was completed by Qutb-ud-din Aibak.

✅ **Qutb-ud-din Aibak** started the construction.
**Iltutmish** completed most of the structure.

---

❌ Ajanta and Ellora are the same cave complex.

✅ **Ajanta Caves** are famous primarily for **Buddhist paintings**.
**Ellora Caves** contain **Hindu, Buddhist and Jain** monuments.

---

❌ Brihadeeswara Temple was built by the Pallavas.

✅ **Brihadeeswara Temple** was built by **Rajaraja Chola I** of the **Chola Dynasty**.
The **Shore Temple** at Mahabalipuram was built by the **Pallavas**.

---

❌ Hampi is located in Tamil Nadu.

✅ **Hampi** is located in **Karnataka** and was the capital of the **Vijayanagara Empire**.
