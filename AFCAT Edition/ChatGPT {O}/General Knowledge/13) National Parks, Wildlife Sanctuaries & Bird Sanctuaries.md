# Background

Protected Areas in India are classified as:

- National Parks
- Wildlife Sanctuaries
- Conservation Reserves
- Community Reserves
- Biosphere Reserves

---

# National Park

Meaning:

Protected area for conserving:

- Wildlife
- Flora
- Fauna
- Ecosystems

Activities like hunting and grazing are generally prohibited.

---

# Wildlife Sanctuary

Meaning:

Protected area mainly for the conservation of wildlife.

Compared to National Parks:

Certain regulated human activities may be permitted.

---

# Biosphere Reserve

Purpose:

Conservation of:

- Biodiversity
- Ecosystems
- Sustainable Development

---

# Tiger Reserve

Established under:

**Project Tiger (1973)**

Purpose:

Conservation of the Bengal Tiger.

---

# Project Tiger

Launched:

**1973**

Purpose:

Protect Bengal Tigers and their habitats.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### National Park ≠ Wildlife Sanctuary

National Park:
- Greater Protection

Wildlife Sanctuary:
- Some Human Activities Allowed

---

### Gir ≠ Kaziranga

- Asiatic Lion

- One-Horned Rhino

- 1973

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Gir National Park is famous for Bengal Tigers.

✅ **Gir National Park** is the **only natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion**.

---

❌ Kaziranga National Park is famous for elephants.

✅ **Kaziranga National Park** is internationally famous for the **Greater One-Horned Rhinoceros**.

---

❌ Jim Corbett is located in Himachal Pradesh.

✅ **Jim Corbett National Park** is located in **Uttarakhand** and is India's **first national park**.

---

❌ Project Tiger was launched under the Wildlife Protection Act in 1992.

✅ **Project Tiger** was launched in **1973**.
The **Wildlife (Protection) Act** was enacted in **1972**.

---

❌ Keibul Lamjao is a bird sanctuary.

✅ **Keibul Lamjao National Park** in **Manipur** is the **world's only floating national park** and is home to the **Sangai deer**.
