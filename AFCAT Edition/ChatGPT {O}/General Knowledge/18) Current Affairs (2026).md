# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ BrahMos is a ballistic missile.

✅ **BrahMos** is a **supersonic cruise missile**.
**Agni-V** is a **ballistic missile**.

---

❌ Tejas is imported from France.

✅ **Tejas** is an **indigenously developed light combat aircraft**.
**Rafale** is imported from **France**.

---

❌ Akash is an air-to-air missile.

✅ **Akash** is a **surface-to-air missile (SAM)**.
**Astra** is an **air-to-air missile**.

---

❌ MALABAR is a bilateral exercise.

✅ **MALABAR** is a **multilateral naval exercise** involving **India, USA, Japan and Australia**.

---

❌ Prachand and Dhruv are the same helicopter.

✅ **Prachand** is a **Light Combat Helicopter (LCH)**.
**Dhruv** is an **Advanced Light Helicopter (ALH)**.
