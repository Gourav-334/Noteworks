# Chapter 9: Dance Forms

> **Purpose:** Study India's classical and major folk dance forms, their states of origin and important characteristics.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT most commonly asks **classical dance ↔ state**, **dance ↔ costume/feature**, and **folk dance ↔ state**.

---

# Background

Indian dances are broadly classified into:

- Classical Dances
- Folk Dances
- Tribal Dances

---

# Classical Dances of India

There are **8 Classical Dance Forms** officially recognized by the **Sangeet Natak Akademi**.

---

# 1. Bharatanatyam

State:

**Tamil Nadu**

Features:

- Oldest classical dance.
- Temple origin.
- Fixed postures and expressions.

---

# 2. Kathak

State:

**Uttar Pradesh**

Features:

- Storytelling tradition.
- Fast footwork.
- Numerous spins (Chakkars).

---

# 3. Kathakali

State:

**Kerala**

Features:

- Elaborate facial makeup.
- Mythological themes.
- Dramatic expressions.

---

# 4. Mohiniyattam

State:

**Kerala**

Features:

- Graceful feminine movements.
- Solo dance.

---

# 5. Kuchipudi

State:

**Andhra Pradesh**

Features:

- Dance-drama tradition.
- Expressive acting.
- Famous for dancing on a brass plate.

---

# 6. Odissi

State:

**Odisha**

Features:

- Tribhangi posture.
- Temple sculptures as inspiration.

---

# 7. Manipuri

State:

**Manipur**

Features:

- Soft and graceful movements.
- Associated with Radha-Krishna devotion.

---

# 8. Sattriya

State:

**Assam**

Features:

- Originated in Vaishnav monasteries (Satras).
- Founded by Srimanta Sankardeva.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Bharatanatyam ≠ Kuchipudi

Bharatanatyam:
- Tamil Nadu

Kuchipudi:
- Andhra Pradesh

---

### Kathak ≠ Kathakali

Kathak:
- Uttar Pradesh
- Fast Footwork

Kathakali:
- Kerala
- Elaborate Makeup

---

### Garba ≠ Ghoomar

Garba:
- Gujarat

Ghoomar:
- Rajasthan

---

### Bhangra ≠ Giddha

Bhangra:
- Traditionally performed by Men

Giddha:
- Traditionally performed by Women

---

### Manipuri ≠ Mohiniyattam

Manipuri:
- Manipur

Mohiniyattam:
- Kerala

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Kathakali originated in Tamil Nadu.

✅ **Kathakali** originated in **Kerala**.
**Bharatanatyam** originated in **Tamil Nadu**.

---

❌ Kuchipudi is the classical dance of Odisha.

✅ **Kuchipudi** belongs to **Andhra Pradesh**.
**Odissi** belongs to **Odisha**.

---

❌ Garba is a folk dance of Rajasthan.

✅ **Garba** is a famous folk dance of **Gujarat**.
**Ghoomar** is associated with **Rajasthan**.

---

❌ There are six classical dance forms in India.

✅ India officially recognizes **eight classical dance forms**.

---

❌ Chhau is performed only in Odisha.

✅ **Chhau** is traditionally performed in parts of **Odisha, Jharkhand and West Bengal**.
