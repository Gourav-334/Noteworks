# Chapter 16: Country – Capital – Currency

> **Purpose:** Study the capitals and currencies of important countries frequently asked in AFCAT and other defence examinations.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT generally asks countries related to **India's neighbours, G20, BRICS, ASEAN, SAARC and major world powers**. Focus on these instead of memorizing every country.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Yen ≠ Yuan

Yen:
- Japan

Yuan (Renminbi):
- China

---

### Riyal ≠ Rial

Riyal:
- Saudi Arabia
- Qatar

Rial:
- Iran
- Oman

---

### Dollar ≠ Euro

Dollar:
- USA, Canada, Australia, Singapore

Euro:
- Many European Union Countries

---

### Pretoria ≠ Cape Town

Pretoria:
- Executive Capital of South Africa

Cape Town:
- Legislative Capital

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The capital of Australia is Sydney.

✅ **Canberra** is the capital of **Australia**.
**Sydney** is its largest city.

---

❌ The capital of Sri Lanka is Colombo.

✅ **Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte** is the **official capital** of Sri Lanka.
**Colombo** is the commercial capital.

---

❌ China's currency is the Yen.

✅ **China's currency** is the **Renminbi (Yuan)**.
**Yen** is the currency of **Japan**.

---

❌ South Africa has only one capital.

✅ South Africa has **three capitals**:
- **Pretoria** (Executive)
- **Cape Town** (Legislative)
- **Bloemfontein** (Judicial)

---

❌ The capital of the Netherlands is The Hague.

✅ **Amsterdam** is the **constitutional capital** of the Netherlands.
**The Hague** is the seat of the government.
