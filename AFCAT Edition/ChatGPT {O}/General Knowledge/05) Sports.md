# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### IOC ≠ FIFA

IOC:
- Olympics

FIFA:
- Football

---

### ICC ≠ BCCI

ICC:
- World Cricket Governing Body

BCCI:
- Indian Cricket Board

---

### Thomas Cup ≠ Uber Cup

Thomas:
- Men's Badminton

Uber:
- Women's Badminton

---

### Wimbledon ≠ French Open

Wimbledon:
- Grass

French Open:
- Clay

---

### Khel Ratna ≠ Arjuna Award

Khel Ratna:
- Highest Sporting Honour

- Outstanding Performance

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ FIFA governs the Olympic Games.

✅ The **Olympic Games** are governed by the **International Olympic Committee (IOC)**.
**FIFA** governs **international football**.

---

❌ Wimbledon is played on a clay court.

✅ **Wimbledon** is played on **grass courts**.
The **French Open** is played on **clay courts**.

---

❌ Thomas Cup is the women's badminton championship.

✅ **Thomas Cup** is the **men's team championship**.
**Uber Cup** is the **women's team championship**.

---

❌ Abhinav Bindra won India's first Olympic medal.

✅ **K. D. Jadhav** won India's **first individual Olympic medal**.
**Abhinav Bindra** won India's **first individual Olympic gold medal**.

---

❌ ICC and BCCI are the same organization.

✅ **ICC** is the **global governing body** for cricket.
**BCCI** administers **cricket in India**.
