# $\fbox{HISTORY}$





## **Topic - 1: Timeline**

- **1857 -** First war of independence where four critical main characters come up as avengers. *Mangal Pandey*, the legend who started the whole revolution. *Rani Laxmibai*, the woman who took the command as the legendary female warrior. *Tantia Tope*, the self-trained guerrilla warrior general. *Bahadur Shah Zafar*, the king who lost his territory to Britishers and wants to claim it back by forming alliance.

- **1885 -** 28 years after the first war of independence, secretly *Indian National Congress* was founded by *Allan Octavian Hume*, a British civil servant in India. And *Womesh Chunder Banerjee*, a long bearded Indian lawyer who practiced law in Britain, and had guts to make an appeal against reduction of British military expenditure.

- **1905 -** Just 5 years after 1900, Bengal was partitioned on basis of religious demographics for election purposes by *Lord Curzon*, and thus the raged citizens started *Swadeshi movement* to boycott British goods. This was led by the *Lal-Bal-Pal* trio.

- **1906 -** Just an year after Bengal partition, two people together created the *All India Muslim League*. Creators were *Khwaja Salimullah*, an elite land owner in states surrounding Bengal region, who specialized in all Arabic languages. And *Aga Khan III*, someone concerned with political recognition of Muslims in India as a separate distinctive community.

- **1907 -** Just an year after *AIML* was formed, Congress session organized at Surat split into leaders having extremist view, and those having moderate view. *Bal Gangadhar Tilak* was leading the group having extremist view, who believed in self-rule as "birthright" and ready to cross limits in order to achieve that. Whereas moderate side led by *Gopal Krishna Gokhle* believed that self-rule must be there, but through co-operation with existing system.

- **1916 -** Lucknow pack under which *Tilak* & *Muhammad Ali Jinnah* came together to form a union between *Congress* and *AIML* in order to overthrow British government in demand of self-rule governance. Moreover, even the moderate and extremist leaders came together under this same objective.

- **1919 -** *Rowlett Act*, also known as *Black Act* was continuation act by *Justice Sydney Rowlett* to continue repressive acts which were introduced during WW1. This act introduced arrests without warrant & imprisonment without trials. Indians were promised political reforms but this act originally set in *1915* still continued. This lead to a peaceful protest in Amritsar which turned into tragic *Jallianwala Bag Massacre* under *General Dyer*.
