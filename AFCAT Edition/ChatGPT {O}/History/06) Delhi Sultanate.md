# Mamluk (Slave) Dynasty

## Qutb-ud-din Aibak

- Founder of the Delhi Sultanate.
- Former slave and general of Muhammad Ghori.
- Known as **"Lakh Baksh"** (Giver of Lakhs) for his generosity.
- Began construction of the **Qutub Minar**.

---

## Iltutmish

Achievements:

- Consolidated the Delhi Sultanate.
- Completed the **Qutub Minar**.
- Introduced the **Silver Tanka** and **Copper Jital** coins.
- Obtained formal recognition from the Abbasid Caliph.

---

## Razia Sultan (1236–1240)

- **First and only female Sultan of Delhi.**
- Daughter of Iltutmish.
- Faced opposition from Turkish nobles.

---

## Balban

Policy:

**"Blood and Iron"**

Achievements:

- Strengthened the monarchy.
- Suppressed revolts.
- Introduced strict court discipline.

---

# Khalji Dynasty

## Jalal-ud-din Khalji

- Founder of the Khalji Dynasty.

---

## Alauddin Khalji

Achievements:

- Defeated the Mongols multiple times.
- Expanded the Sultanate into South India.
- Introduced market reforms.
- Strengthened the army.

---

## Market Reforms

Introduced:

- Price control
- State-regulated markets
- Grain storage
- Strict market supervision

Purpose:

- Maintain a large standing army at lower cost.

---

## Military Reforms

- Branding of horses (**Dagh**)
- Descriptive rolls of soldiers (**Chehra**)

---

# Tughlaq Dynasty

## Ghiyas-ud-din Tughlaq

- Founder of the Tughlaq Dynasty.

---

## Muhammad bin Tughlaq

Known for ambitious but often unsuccessful experiments.

Major policies:

- Transfer of capital from Delhi to Daulatabad.
- Introduction of Token Currency.
- Increased taxation in the Doab.
- Expedition to Khurasan (planned but abandoned).

> Often remembered as an intelligent ruler whose policies failed in execution.

---

## Firoz Shah Tughlaq

Achievements:

- Constructed canals.
- Built new towns.
- Encouraged agriculture.
- Established charitable institutions.

---

# Sayyid Dynasty

Founder:

**Khizr Khan**

Features:

- Ruled after Timur's invasion.
- Comparatively weak central authority.

---

# Lodi Dynasty

## Bahlul Lodi

- Founder of the Lodi Dynasty.

---

## Sikandar Lodi

Achievements:

- Founded the city of **Agra**.
- Encouraged agriculture.
- Improved administration.

---

## Ibrahim Lodi

- Last Sultan of Delhi.
- Defeated by Babur in the **First Battle of Panipat (1526)**.

---

# Administration

Main features:

- Centralized government.
- Iqta system.
- Standing army.
- Efficient revenue administration.

---

# Iqta System

An **Iqta** was a land assignment.

Holder:

**Muqti (or Iqtadar)**

Responsibilities:

- Collect revenue.
- Maintain law and order.
- Maintain troops.

---

# Economy

Main source of revenue:

- Land tax

Major activities:

- Agriculture
- Trade
- Handicrafts

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Qutb-ud-din Aibak ≠ Iltutmish

Aibak:
- Founded Delhi Sultanate
- Started Qutub Minar

Iltutmish:
- Completed Qutub Minar
- Introduced Tanka & Jital

---

### Alauddin Khalji ≠ Muhammad bin Tughlaq

Alauddin:
- Market reforms
- Dagh & Chehra

Muhammad bin Tughlaq:
- Token Currency
- Shifted capital
- Doab taxation

---

### Balban ≠ Alauddin Khalji

Balban:
- Blood and Iron
- Strong monarchy

Alauddin:
- Economic & military reforms
- Southern expansion

---

### Sikandar Lodi ≠ Ibrahim Lodi

Sikandar:
- Founded Agra

Ibrahim:
- Defeated by Babur
