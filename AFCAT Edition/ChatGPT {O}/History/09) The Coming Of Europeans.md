# Arrival of Europeans

## Portuguese

First Europeans to establish a permanent presence in India.

### Vasco da Gama

- Reached **Calicut (Kozhikode)** in **1498**.
- Landed with the help of the Arab navigator **Ahmad ibn Majid** *(traditional account; modern historians debate this attribution)*.
- Welcomed by:
  - **Zamorin (Samoothiri) of Calicut**

---

## Francisco de Almeida

First Portuguese Governor in India.

Policy:

**Blue Water Policy**

Main objective:

- Control sea routes and naval dominance.

---

## Afonso de Albuquerque

Considered the **real founder of the Portuguese Empire in India**.

Achievements:

- Captured **Goa (1510)**.
- Encouraged permanent Portuguese settlements.
- Strengthened naval power.

---

# Battle of Diu (1509)

Between:

- Portuguese
- Combined fleet of Egypt, Gujarat and Calicut

Result:

- Portuguese naval supremacy in the Indian Ocean.

---

# Dutch

**1602**

- Spice trade

# Danish

Established settlements at:

- Tranquebar (Tamil Nadu)
- Serampore (West Bengal)

Eventually sold their possessions to the British.

---

# Carnatic Wars (1746–1763)

Between:

- British
- French

Purpose:

- Control over South India.

Result:

- British emerged victorious.
- French political influence declined significantly.

---

# Battle of Plassey (1757)

Date:

**23 June 1757**

Between:

- British East India Company
- Siraj-ud-Daulah

British Commander:

**Robert Clive**

Reason for British victory:

- Betrayal by **Mir Jafar** and other conspirators.

Result:

- Beginning of British political supremacy in India.

---

# Battle of Buxar (1764)

Between:

British East India Company

vs

- Mir Qasim
- Shuja-ud-Daulah
- Shah Alam II

British Commander:

**Hector Munro**

Result:

- Decisive British victory.

---

# Treaty of Allahabad (1765)

Signed between:

- Robert Clive
- Mughal Emperor Shah Alam II

Result:

The British received:

**Diwani Rights**

(Revenue collection rights in Bengal, Bihar and Orissa.)

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Plassey ≠ Buxar

Plassey:
- 1757
- Siraj-ud-Daulah
- Robert Clive
- Political beginning

Buxar:
- 1764
- Mir Qasim + Allies
- Hector Munro
- Military supremacy

---

### Almeida ≠ Albuquerque

Almeida:
- Blue Water Policy
- First Governor

Albuquerque:
- Captured Goa
- Permanent settlements

---

### Hawkins ≠ Sir Thomas Roe

William Hawkins:
- First English envoy
- Jahangir

Sir Thomas Roe:
- Official ambassador
- Trading privileges

---

### Portuguese ≠ English

Portuguese:
- 1498
- Goa

English:
- 1600
- Surat
- Calcutta
- Madras
- Bombay

❌ Vasco da Gama landed at Goa.

✅ He landed at **Calicut (Kozhikode)**.

---

❌ Robert Clive fought the Battle of Buxar.

✅ **Robert Clive** led the British at **Plassey**.

---

❌ The Portuguese established the English East India Company.

✅ The **English East India Company** was chartered by **Queen Elizabeth I** in **1600**.

---

❌ Diwani Rights were obtained after the Battle of Plassey.

✅ They were granted in **1765** after the **Battle of Buxar** through the **Treaty of Allahabad**.

---

❌ The French captured Goa.

✅ **Goa** was captured by the **Portuguese** under **Afonso de Albuquerque**.
