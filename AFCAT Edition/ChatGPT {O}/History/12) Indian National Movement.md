# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Champaran ≠ Kheda ≠ Ahmedabad

Champaran:
- Indigo
- Bihar
- First Satyagraha

Kheda:
- Tax Relief
- Gujarat

Ahmedabad:
- Mill Workers
- Gujarat

---

### Non-Cooperation ≠ Civil Disobedience ≠ Quit India

Non-Cooperation:
- 1920

Civil Disobedience:
- 1930

Quit India:
- 1942

---

### INC Founder ≠ First President

Founder:
- A. O. Hume

First President:
- W. C. Bonnerjee

---

### Rash Behari Bose ≠ Subhas Chandra Bose

Rash Behari Bose:
- Founded INA

Subhas Chandra Bose:
- Reorganized and led INA

---

### Simon Commission ≠ Nehru Report

Simon Commission:
- British Commission
- No Indian members

Nehru Report:
- Indian constitutional proposal
- Chaired by Motilal Nehru

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Gandhi's first movement in India was the Dandi March.

✅ It was the **Champaran Satyagraha (1917)**.

---

❌ The Simon Commission included Indian representatives.

✅ **No Indian** was a member of the Simon Commission.

---

❌ The Quit India Movement started in 1940.

✅ It was launched on **8 August 1942**.

---

❌ The INA was founded by Subhas Chandra Bose.

✅ It was **founded by Rash Behari Bose** and later **reorganized and led by Subhas Chandra Bose**.

---

❌ The first President of the INC was A. O. Hume.

✅ **A. O. Hume** founded the INC, while **W. C. Bonnerjee** was its **first President**.
