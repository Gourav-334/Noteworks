# Humayun (1530–1556)

Achievements:

- Lost the empire to Sher Shah Suri.
- Regained Delhi in 1555.
- Died after falling from the stairs of his library.

---

# Sher Shah Suri (Interregnum)

Although not a Mughal, he made significant administrative reforms.

Achievements:

- Introduced the **Rupiya**.
- Reorganized land revenue.
- Improved the **Grand Trunk Road**.
- Built **Purana Qila** (completed during his reign).
- Efficient postal system.

> **Sher Shah Suri is frequently asked in AFCAT.**

---

# Aurangzeb (1658–1707)

Longest reigning Mughal emperor.

Achievements:

- Maximum territorial expansion.
- Annexed Bijapur and Golconda.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### First Panipat ≠ Second Panipat

1526:
- Babur vs Ibrahim Lodi

1556:
- Akbar (Bairam Khan) vs Hemu

---

### Akbarnama ≠ Ain-i-Akbari

Both written by:

**Abul Fazl**

Akbarnama:
- History of Akbar's reign

Ain-i-Akbari:
- Administration and governance

---

### Mansabdari ≠ Dahsala

Mansabdari:
- Military & civil ranking system

Dahsala:
- Revenue settlement

---

### Baburnama ≠ Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri

Baburnama:
- Babur

Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri:
- Jahangir

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Din-i-Ilahi was founded by Jahangir.

✅ It was founded by **Akbar** in **1582**.

---

❌ Baburnama was written in Persian.

✅ It was originally written in **Chagatai Turkish**.

---

❌ Sir Thomas Roe visited Akbar's court.

✅ He visited **Jahangir's** court.

---

❌ Sher Shah Suri was a Mughal emperor.

✅ He belonged to the **Sur Dynasty** but is studied alongside the Mughals due to his important administrative reforms.

---

❌ Taj Mahal was built by Akbar.

✅ It was built by **Shah Jahan** in memory of **Mumtaz Mahal**.
