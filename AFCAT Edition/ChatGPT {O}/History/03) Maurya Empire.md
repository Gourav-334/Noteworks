# Chapter 3: Maurya Empire (c. 322–185 BCE)

> **Founder:** Chandragupta Maurya
>
> **Capital:** Pataliputra
>
> **Greatest Ruler:** Ashoka
>
> **First Empire to politically unify most of the Indian subcontinent**

---

# Background

- Founded after overthrowing the **Nanda Dynasty**.
- With the guidance of **Chanakya (Kautilya/Vishnugupta)**, Chandragupta established India's first large centralized empire.

---

# Important Rulers

## Chandragupta Maurya (322–298 BCE)

- Founder of the Maurya Empire.
- Defeated the last Nanda ruler.
- Defeated **Seleucus I Nicator** (Successor of Alexander).
- Signed a peace treaty with Seleucus.

### Treaty with Seleucus

Seleucus ceded:

- Arachosia
- Gedrosia
- Aria
- Paropamisadae

In return:

- Received **500 war elephants**
- Marriage alliance (according to classical sources)

---

## Bindusara (298–273 BCE)

- Son of Chandragupta.
- Expanded the empire southward.
- Greek sources call him **Amitrochates** (Destroyer of enemies).

Greek ambassadors during his reign:

- Deimachus
- Dionysius

---

## Ashoka (268–232 BCE)

- Greatest Mauryan ruler.
- Initially followed military expansion.
- After the **Kalinga War**, adopted **Dhamma**.

---

# Kalinga War (261 BCE)

Location:

- Present-day Odisha

According to Ashoka's inscriptions:

- Around **100,000 killed**
- Many more wounded and displaced

Result:

- Ashoka renounced aggressive warfare.
- Promoted peace and moral governance.

---

# Ashoka's Dhamma

Main principles:

- Non-violence
- Religious tolerance
- Respect for elders
- Compassion
- Welfare of people
- Welfare of animals

> **Dhamma was a moral code, not a new religion.**

---

# Arthashastra

Author:

**Kautilya (Chanakya)**

Topics covered:

- Administration
- Economy
- Espionage
- Diplomacy
- Warfare
- Taxation

> It is one of the most important works on statecraft in ancient India.

---

# Megasthenes

- Greek ambassador sent by **Seleucus I**.
- Stayed in the court of **Chandragupta Maurya**.

Book:

**Indica**

Provides information on:

- Administration
- Society
- Economy
- Pataliputra

---

# Economy

Main occupation:

- Agriculture

Other features:

- State-controlled economy
- Efficient taxation
- Roads for trade
- Irrigation systems
- Punch-marked coins in circulation

---

# Society

- Four-fold Varna system continued.
- Slavery as understood in the Greco-Roman world was not widespread.
- Cities and trade expanded significantly.

---

# Religion

### Chandragupta Maurya

According to Jain tradition:

- Accepted **Jainism** later in life.
- Died at **Shravanabelagola** through **Sallekhana** (ritual fasting).

---

### Ashoka

- Accepted **Buddhism** after the Kalinga War.
- Supported the spread of Buddhism inside and outside India.

---

# Buddhist Missions

Ashoka sent missionaries to:

- Sri Lanka
- Nepal
- Central Asia
- Southeast Asia

His son:

**Mahinda**

His daughter:

**Sanghamitta**

Both helped spread Buddhism in **Sri Lanka**.

---

# Ashokan Inscriptions

Languages:

- Prakrit (most inscriptions)
- Greek
- Aramaic

Scripts:

- Brahmi (most of India)
- Kharosthi (North-West)

> **Brahmi script was deciphered by James Prinsep in 1837.**

---

# Edicts

Types:

- Major Rock Edicts
- Minor Rock Edicts
- Pillar Edicts

Purpose:

- Communicate Dhamma
- Administrative instructions
- Welfare measures

---

# Lion Capital of Sarnath

Features:

- Four Asiatic lions
- Ashoka Chakra below the lions

Importance:

- National Emblem of India
- Ashoka Chakra appears on the Indian National Flag.

---

# Art & Architecture

Important monuments:

- Sanchi Stupa
- Barabar Caves
- Ashokan Pillars

Characteristics:

- Highly polished sandstone
- Monolithic pillars
- Animal capitals

---

# Decline

Possible reasons:

- Weak successors
- Administrative difficulties
- Large empire
- Economic strain
- Provincial revolts

Last Mauryan ruler:

**Brihadratha**

Killed by:

**Pushyamitra Shunga**

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Megasthenes ≠ Faxian ≠ Hiuen Tsang

- Megasthenes → Chandragupta Maurya
- Faxian → Gupta Empire
- Hiuen Tsang → Harshavardhana

---

### Arthashastra ≠ Indica

Arthashastra:
- Written by **Kautilya**

Indica:
- Written by **Megasthenes**

---

### Dhamma ≠ Buddhism

Dhamma:
- Ethical and administrative moral code

Buddhism:
- Religion founded by Gautama Buddha

---

### Brahmi ≠ Kharosthi

Brahmi:
- Most of India

Kharosthi:
- North-West India
