# Chapter 2: Vedic Period (c. 1500–600 BCE)

> **Also Known As:** Vedic Age
>
> **Main Sources:** The Four Vedas
>
> **Major Phases:**
> - Early (Rig Vedic) Period
> - Later Vedic Period

---

# Timeline

| Period | Years |
|---------|--------|
| Early (Rig Vedic) | c. 1500–1000 BCE |
| Later Vedic | c. 1000–600 BCE |

---

# Origin

- Associated with the arrival of the **Indo-Aryans**.
- Initially settled in **Punjab and the Sapta Sindhu (Land of Seven Rivers)**.
- Later expanded eastward into the **Ganga-Yamuna Doab**.

---

|------|-------|

| Atharvaveda | Magic, medicine, daily life |

## Other Important Texts

- Brahmanas
- Aranyakas

---

# Political Organization

### Early Vedic

- Tribe (**Jana**) was the basic political unit.
- King was called **Rajan**.
- Kingship was **not fully hereditary**.

Popular assemblies:

- Sabha
- Samiti
- Vidatha

---

### Later Vedic

- Kingdoms became larger.
- Kingship became hereditary.
- Royal authority increased.
- Assemblies gradually lost importance.

---

# Society

### Early Vedic Society

- Mostly tribal and pastoral.
- Comparatively equal society.
- Women enjoyed relatively higher status.
- Inter-caste restrictions were minimal.

---

### Later Vedic Society

- Agriculture became dominant.
- Society became more hierarchical.
- Position of women declined.
- Varna system became rigid.

---

# Varna System

Initially based on **occupation**, later became **birth-based**.

| Varna | Function |
|--------|----------|
| Brahmin | Priests |
| Kshatriya | Warriors |
| Vaishya | Traders & Farmers |
| Shudra | Service providers |

---

# Family

- Family was called **Kula**.
- Father was head of the household.
- Joint family system was common.

---

# Economy

## Early Vedic

Main occupation:

- Cattle rearing

Wealth measured by:

- Number of cattle

Agriculture existed but was secondary.

---

## Later Vedic

Agriculture became the backbone.

Major crops:

- Rice
- Wheat
- Barley

New developments:

- Iron tools
- Larger settlements
- Increased trade

---

# Iron

Iron became common during the **Later Vedic Period**.

Known as:

**Shyama Ayas (Black Metal)**

It helped in:

- Forest clearing
- Agriculture
- Expansion of settlements

# Women in Vedic Society

Early Vedic:

- Could attend assemblies.
- Received education.
- Participated in religious rituals.

Later Vedic:

- Status gradually declined.
- Child marriage became more common towards the end of the period.
- Property rights became more restricted.

---

# Transition to Mahajanapadas

By around **600 BCE**:

- Small tribal kingdoms evolved into larger territorial states.
- This marked the beginning of the **Mahajanapada Period**.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Early Vedic ≠ Later Vedic

Early:
- Pastoral economy
- Tribal society
- Flexible Varna system
- Better status of women

Later:
- Agriculture
- Large kingdoms
- Hereditary monarchy
- Rigid Varna system

---

### Rigveda ≠ Samaveda

Rigveda:
- Hymns

Samaveda:
- Music and chants

---

### Yajurveda ≠ Atharvaveda

Yajurveda:
- Sacrificial rituals

Atharvaveda:
- Magic, medicine, healing

---

### Sabha ≠ Samiti

Both were popular assemblies.

Generally:
- Sabha → Council of elders/nobles
- Samiti → Larger popular tribal assembly
