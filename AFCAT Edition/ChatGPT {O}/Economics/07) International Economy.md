# Background

Countries cooperate through international organizations to:

- Promote trade
- Maintain financial stability
- Reduce poverty
- Support economic development

---

# International Monetary Fund (IMF)

Established:

**1944**

Headquarters:

**Washington, D.C., USA**

Purpose:

- Promote international monetary cooperation.
- Maintain exchange rate stability.
- Provide financial assistance to member countries facing balance of payments crises.

Members:

Nearly all sovereign countries.

---

# World Bank

Established:

**1944**

Headquarters:

**Washington, D.C., USA**

Purpose:

- Reduce poverty.
- Finance development projects.
- Improve infrastructure.

Major Institutions:

- IBRD
- IDA

---

# IMF vs World Bank

| IMF | World Bank |
|------|------------|
| Monetary Stability | Development Projects |
| Short-Term Financial Assistance | Long-Term Development Loans |
| Balance of Payments | Poverty Reduction |

---

# World Trade Organization (WTO)

Established:

**1995**

Headquarters:

**Geneva, Switzerland**

Replaced:

**General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT)**

Purpose:

- Promote international trade.
- Reduce trade barriers.
- Resolve trade disputes.

---

# GATT

Full Form:

**General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade**

Established:

**1947**

Replaced by:

**WTO (1995)**

---

# BRICS

Originally:

- Brazil
- Russia
- India
- China

Later Added:

- South Africa (2010)

Since 2024, the grouping has expanded further to include additional member countries.

Purpose:

- Economic Cooperation
- Trade
- Development
- Reform of Global Financial Institutions

---

# New Development Bank (NDB)

Also called:

**BRICS Bank**

Established:

**2014**

Headquarters:

**Shanghai, China**

Purpose:

Infrastructure and sustainable development financing.

---

# G20

Meaning:

Group of Twenty.

Members:

19 Countries

+

European Union

+

African Union *(from 2023)*

Purpose:

- Global Economic Cooperation
- Financial Stability
- Sustainable Growth

India hosted the:

**2023 G20 Summit (New Delhi).**

---

# G7

Members:

- Canada
- France
- Germany
- Italy
- Japan
- United Kingdom
- United States

Purpose:

Cooperation among advanced economies.

---

# ASEAN

Full Form:

**Association of Southeast Asian Nations**

Established:

**1967**

Headquarters:

**Jakarta, Indonesia**

Members:

10 Southeast Asian countries.

Purpose:

- Economic Growth
- Regional Cooperation
- Peace

---

# SAARC

Full Form:

**South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation**

Established:

**1985**

Headquarters:

**Kathmandu, Nepal**

Members:

8 Countries

- Afghanistan
- Bangladesh
- Bhutan
- India
- Maldives
- Nepal
- Pakistan
- Sri Lanka

Purpose:

Regional Cooperation in South Asia.

---

# BIMSTEC

Full Form:

**Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation**

Members:

7 Countries around the Bay of Bengal.

Purpose:

Regional economic and technical cooperation.

> **Important because it does not include Pakistan.**

---

# Asian Development Bank (ADB)

Established:

**1966**

Headquarters:

**Manila, Philippines**

Purpose:

Economic development in Asia and the Pacific.

---

# Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)

Established:

**2016 (Operations Began)**

Headquarters:

**Beijing, China**

Purpose:

Infrastructure financing.

India is one of the largest shareholders.

---

# Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)

Headquarters:

**Paris, France**

Purpose:

Economic policy coordination among mostly developed countries.

India is **not** a member.

---

# United Nations (UN)

Established:

**1945**

Headquarters:

**New York, USA**

Purpose:

- Peace
- Security
- Development
- International Cooperation

---

# Balance of Payments (BoP)

Meaning:

Record of all economic transactions between:

Residents of a country

and

the rest of the world.

Components:

- Current Account
- Capital Account

---

# Foreign Exchange Reserves

Meaning:

Foreign currencies and assets held by the country's central bank.

Purpose:

- Support imports.
- Maintain currency stability.
- Meet international obligations.

Managed in India by:

**RBI**

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### IMF ≠ World Bank

IMF:
- Monetary Stability
- Balance of Payments

World Bank:
- Development Projects
- Poverty Reduction

---

### WTO ≠ GATT

GATT:
- Agreement (1947)

WTO:
- Organization (1995)

---

### ASEAN ≠ SAARC

ASEAN:
- Southeast Asia

SAARC:
- South Asia

---

### ADB ≠ AIIB

ADB:
- Manila

AIIB:
- Beijing

---

### BRICS ≠ G7

BRICS:
- Major Emerging Economies

G7:
- Advanced Industrialized Economies

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The IMF provides long-term infrastructure loans.

✅ The **IMF** mainly provides **financial assistance for balance of payments problems**.
The **World Bank** finances **long-term development projects**.

---

❌ WTO and GATT are the same organization.

✅ **GATT** was an agreement established in **1947**.
It was replaced by the **WTO** in **1995**.

---

❌ SAARC headquarters is in New Delhi.

✅ **SAARC Headquarters** is located in **Kathmandu, Nepal**.

---

❌ ASEAN is a South Asian organization.

✅ **ASEAN** represents **Southeast Asian nations**.
**SAARC** represents **South Asian countries**.

---

❌ AIIB headquarters is in Manila.

✅ **AIIB Headquarters** is in **Beijing, China**.
**ADB Headquarters** is in **Manila, Philippines**.
