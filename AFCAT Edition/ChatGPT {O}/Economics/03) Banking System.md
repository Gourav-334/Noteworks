# Background

A bank is a financial institution that:

- Accepts deposits.
- Gives loans.
- Facilitates payments.
- Creates credit.

The banking system is regulated by:

**Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**

---

# Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Established:

**1 April 1935**

Nationalised:

**1 January 1949**

Headquarters:

**Mumbai**

Type:

**Central Bank of India**

---

# Functions of RBI

## Monetary Authority

Controls:

- Money Supply
- Credit

---

## Banker to Government

Maintains accounts of:

- Central Government
- State Governments

---

## Banker's Bank

Provides financial support to:

Commercial Banks.

---

## Currency Issuer

Issues:

Indian Currency Notes

> Except **₹1 note** and coins, which are issued by the **Government of India**.

---

## Foreign Exchange Manager

Manages:

Foreign Exchange Reserves.

Implements:

**FEMA, 1999**

---

## Financial Stability

Regulates:

- Banks
- NBFCs
- Payment Systems

---

# Bank Rate

Rate at which:

RBI lends money to commercial banks for the long term.

---

# Repo Rate

Meaning:

Rate at which RBI lends money to commercial banks against government securities.

Purpose:

- Increase or decrease liquidity.
- Control inflation.

---

# Reverse Repo Rate

Meaning:

Rate at which RBI borrows money from commercial banks.

Purpose:

Absorb excess liquidity.

---

# Scheduled Banks

Included in:

**Second Schedule of the RBI Act, 1934**

Eligible for:

- RBI financial assistance.
- RBI facilities.

Examples:

- Public Sector Banks
- Private Banks
- Foreign Banks
- Regional Rural Banks

---

# Non-Scheduled Banks

Not included in:

Second Schedule of RBI Act.

Generally:

Smaller banks.

Limited RBI facilities.

---

# Digital Payment Systems

---

## NEFT

Full Form:

**National Electronic Funds Transfer**

Features:

- Nationwide fund transfer.
- No minimum amount.
- Available 24×7.

---

## RTGS

Full Form:

**Real Time Gross Settlement**

Features:

- Real-time settlement.
- Used mainly for large-value transfers.
- Minimum amount: **₹2 lakh**

---

## IMPS

Full Form:

**Immediate Payment Service**

Features:

- Instant transfer.
- Available 24×7.

---

## UPI

Full Form:

**Unified Payments Interface**

Developed by:

**NPCI**

Features:

- Instant payments.
- Mobile-based.
- QR Code support.
- 24×7.

---

# NPCI

Full Form:

**National Payments Corporation of India**

Responsible for:

- UPI
- RuPay
- IMPS
- NACH

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### RBI ≠ Commercial Bank

RBI:
- Central Bank
- Regulator

Commercial Bank:
- Customer Banking

---

### Repo ≠ Reverse Repo

Repo:
- RBI lends to Banks

Reverse Repo:
- RBI borrows from Banks

---

### Debit Card ≠ Credit Card

Debit:
- Own Money

Credit:
- Borrowed Money

---

### NEFT ≠ RTGS

NEFT:
- No Minimum
- Suitable for all transfers

RTGS:
- ₹2 Lakh Minimum
- High-value transfers

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ RBI prints every Indian currency note.

✅ **₹1 note** is issued by the **Government of India**.
Other currency notes are issued by the **RBI**.

---

❌ Repo Rate is the rate at which banks lend to customers.

✅ **Repo Rate** is the rate at which the **RBI lends to commercial banks** against government securities.

---

❌ RTGS can be used for transfers of any amount.

✅ **RTGS** is primarily for **high-value transfers** with a **minimum transaction amount of ₹2 lakh**.

---

❌ UPI is operated directly by the RBI.

✅ **UPI** was developed and is operated by the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)** under RBI oversight.

---

❌ Scheduled Banks and Commercial Banks mean the same thing.

✅ Most commercial banks are **scheduled banks**, but **"scheduled"** refers specifically to inclusion in the **Second Schedule of the RBI Act, 1934**.
