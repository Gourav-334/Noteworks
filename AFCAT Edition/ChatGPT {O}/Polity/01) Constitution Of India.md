# Historical Background

The Constitution evolved through several British Acts.

## Regulating Act (1773)

- First major constitutional law for British India.
- Established the office of Governor-General of Bengal.

---

## Government of India Act (1858)

- Ended East India Company rule.
- Crown Rule began.

---

## Government of India Act (1909)

- Introduced separate electorates.

---

## Government of India Act (1919)

- Introduced Dyarchy in provinces.

---

## Government of India Act (1935)

Most important constitutional act before independence.

Introduced:

- Provincial Autonomy
- Federal Scheme (never fully implemented)
- Federal Court
- Public Service Commissions

> **A large part of the Indian Constitution is based on the Government of India Act, 1935.**

# Preamble

The Preamble begins with:

> **"We, the People of India..."**

It declares India to be:

- Sovereign
- Socialist
- Secular
- Democratic
- Republic

It aims to secure:

- Justice
- Liberty
- Equality
- Fraternity

---

## Important Amendments to the Preamble

### 42nd Constitutional Amendment (1976)

Added:

- Socialist

# Features of the Indian Constitution

- Written Constitution
- Lengthiest written Constitution
- Federal system with unitary features
- Parliamentary system
- Independent Judiciary
- Fundamental Rights
- Directive Principles
- Fundamental Duties
- Single Citizenship
- Universal Adult Franchise
- Rule of Law
- Secular State

---

# Sources of the Constitution

| Country | Borrowed Feature |
|----------|------------------|
| United Kingdom | Parliamentary System, Rule of Law, Cabinet System |
| United States | Fundamental Rights, Judicial Review, Impeachment, Vice President |
| Ireland | Directive Principles of State Policy |
| Canada | Strong Centre, Residuary Powers |
| Australia | Concurrent List, Joint Sitting |
| Germany (Weimar) | Emergency Provisions |
| USSR (Russia) | Fundamental Duties, Five-Year Plans (historically) |
| South Africa | Amendment Procedure, Election of Rajya Sabha Members |
| Japan | Procedure established by law (Article 21 concept of due procedure influenced later interpretation; **"Procedure Established by Law"** directly borrowed from the Japanese Constitution.) |

# Citizenship

The Constitution initially provided for **single citizenship**.

Citizenship provisions are contained in:

**Articles 5–11**

Further governed by:

**Citizenship Act, 1955**

---

# Rule of Law

Borrowed from:

**United Kingdom**

Given by:

**A. V. Dicey**

Meaning:

- Equality before law.
- No arbitrary exercise of power.

---

# Universal Adult Franchise

Every citizen aged:

**18 years or above**

has the right to vote.

> Voting age was reduced from **21 to 18 years** by the **61st Constitutional Amendment (1988).**

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

✅ **Dr. B. R. Ambedkar** was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

---

❌ The Constitution came into force on 26 November 1949.

✅ It was **adopted** on **26 November 1949** and **came into force** on **26 January 1950**.

---

❌ The Constitution is mainly based on the British Constitution.

✅ While it borrows features from many countries, **the Government of India Act, 1935** is its **largest single source**.

---

❌ The terms "Socialist" and "Secular" were part of the original Constitution.

✅ They were added by the **42nd Constitutional Amendment (1976)**.

---

❌ Voting age in India has always been 18 years.

✅ It was reduced from **21 years to 18 years** by the **61st Constitutional Amendment (1988)**.
