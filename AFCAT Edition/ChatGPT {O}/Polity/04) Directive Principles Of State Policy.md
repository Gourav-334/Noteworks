# Chapter 4: Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)

> **Part:** IV
>
> **Articles:** 36–51
>
> **Borrowed From:** Constitution of Ireland
>
> **Nature:** Non-Justiciable (Not enforceable by courts)
>
> **Objective:** Establish a Welfare State in India.

---

# Timeline

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1950 | DPSPs came into force |
| 1976 | 42nd Amendment added new DPSPs |
| 2002 | 86th Amendment modified Article 45 |

---

# What are DPSPs?

Directive Principles of State Policy are:

- Guidelines to the Government.
- Not enforceable in courts.
- Fundamental in the governance of the country.
- Intended to establish social and economic democracy.

---

# Purpose

To establish:

- Social Justice
- Economic Justice
- Political Justice
- Welfare State

# Important DPSPs

## Article 39

State shall strive for:

- Equal livelihood opportunities
- Equal pay for equal work
- Protection of children
- Distribution of wealth for public good

---

## Article 39A

Provides:

Free Legal Aid

Added by:

**42nd Constitutional Amendment (1976)**

---

## Article 40

State shall organize:

**Village Panchayats**

Basis for:

**73rd Constitutional Amendment**

---

## Article 42

Provides:

- Humane working conditions
- Maternity relief

---

## Article 44

Uniform Civil Code (UCC)

Purpose:

A common civil law for all citizens regardless of religion.

> **UCC remains a Directive Principle, not a Fundamental Right.**

---

## Article 45

Originally:

Free & compulsory education up to 14 years.

After the **86th Amendment (2002):**

Early childhood care and education for children below **6 years**.

---

## Article 46

Promotion of:

- Educational interests
- Economic interests

of:

- SCs
- STs
- Other Weaker Sections

---

## Article 47

Duty of the State:

- Improve nutrition
- Improve public health
- Prohibit intoxicating drinks harmful to health

---

## Article 48A

Protection of:

- Environment
- Forests
- Wildlife

Added by:

**42nd Amendment**

---

## Article 50

Separation of:

Judiciary

from

Executive

---

## Article 51

Promotion of:

- International Peace
- International Security
- Respect for International Law

# Difference Between Fundamental Rights & DPSPs

| Fundamental Rights | DPSPs |
|--------------------|--------|
| Justiciable | Non-Justiciable |
| Legal Rights | Constitutional Guidelines |
| Protect Individual | Promote Welfare State |
| Negative & Positive Obligations | Mostly Positive Obligations |
| Part III | Part IV |

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Fundamental Rights ≠ DPSPs

Fundamental Rights:
- Enforceable
- Part III

DPSPs:
- Not enforceable
- Part IV

---

### Article 39A ≠ Article 32

Article 39A:
- Free Legal Aid

Article 32:
- Constitutional Remedies

---

### Article 40 ≠ Article 243

Article 40:
- DPSP
- Village Panchayats

Article 243:
- Constitutional provisions relating to Panchayats (added by the 73rd Amendment)

---

### Article 45 ≠ Article 21A

Article 45:
- Early Childhood Care (<6 years)

Article 21A:
- Education (6–14 years)

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ DPSPs are enforceable in courts.

✅ DPSPs are **Non-Justiciable**.

---

❌ DPSPs were borrowed from the USA.

✅ They were borrowed from the **Constitution of Ireland**.

---

❌ Uniform Civil Code is a Fundamental Right.

✅ It is a **Directive Principle under Article 44**.

---

❌ Article 48A deals with Wildlife Protection as a Fundamental Duty.

✅ **Article 48A** is a **DPSP** dealing with **Environment, Forests and Wildlife**.

---

❌ Article 45 and Article 21A both provide education for children aged 6–14 years.

✅ **Article 21A** provides **free and compulsory education (6–14 years)**.
**Article 45** provides **early childhood care and education for children below 6 years**.
