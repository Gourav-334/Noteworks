# The President

## Constitutional Position

- Head of the State.
- First Citizen of India.
- Nominal (Constitutional) Executive.
- Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces.

> **Real executive power is exercised by the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers.**

---

| 61 | Impeachment |

---

## Election

Elected by:

Electoral College consisting of:

- Elected MPs of Lok Sabha
- Elected MPs of Rajya Sabha
- Elected MLAs of States
- Elected MLAs of Delhi & Puducherry

---

## Qualifications

- Citizen of India
- Minimum age: **35 years**
- Qualified to become Lok Sabha member
- Must not hold an office of profit

---

## Term

- Five years
- Eligible for re-election

---

## Oath

Administered by:

**Chief Justice of India**

(In his absence, the senior-most judge of the Supreme Court.)

---

## Removal

Method:

**Impeachment**

Ground:

Violation of the Constitution.

---

# Powers of the President

## Executive Powers

- Appoints Prime Minister.
- Appoints Governors.
- Appoints Chief Justice of India.
- Appoints Election Commissioners.
- Appoints CAG.
- Appoints Attorney General.

---

## Legislative Powers

- Summons Parliament.
- Prorogues Parliament.
- Dissolves Lok Sabha.
- Addresses Parliament.
- Gives assent to Bills.
- Can promulgate Ordinances.

---

## Financial Powers

- Budget introduced with President's recommendation.
- Money Bill requires President's recommendation before introduction.

---

## Judicial Powers

- Grant Pardon
- Reprieve
- Respite
- Remission
- Commutation

Under:

**Article 72**

---

## Military Powers

Supreme Commander of:

- Army
- Navy
- Air Force

---

## Diplomatic Powers

- Appoints Ambassadors.
- Receives Foreign Diplomats.
- Represents India internationally.

---

# Veto Powers

| Veto | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Absolute Veto | Rejects Bill completely |
| Suspensive Veto | Returns Bill for reconsideration |
| Pocket Veto | Takes no action indefinitely |

> **Pocket Veto** has been used only rarely in India.

---

# Ordinance

Article:

**123**

Issued by:

President

Conditions:

- Parliament not in session.
- Same force as an Act of Parliament.

63–71

# Prime Minister

Article:

**75**

Position:

- Real Executive
- Head of Government

Appointed by:

President

Usually:

Leader of the majority party in Lok Sabha.

---

# Powers of Prime Minister

- Heads Council of Ministers.
- Advises President.
- Coordinates ministries.
- Leader of Lok Sabha (if a member).
- Principal policy-maker.

---

# Council of Ministers

Articles:

74–75

Purpose:

Aid and advise the President.

---

## Three Categories

- Cabinet Ministers
- Ministers of State
- Deputy Ministers

> **Cabinet Ministers form the core decision-making body.**

# Types of Bills

## Ordinary Bill

Can be introduced in:

- Either House

---

## Money Bill

Article:

110

Can be introduced:

**Only in Lok Sabha**

Requires:

President's recommendation.

Speaker decides whether a Bill is a Money Bill.

Rajya Sabha:

Can recommend changes but cannot reject it.

Must return within:

**14 days**

---

## Financial Bill

Differs from Money Bill.

Can involve broader financial matters.

---

## Constitutional Amendment Bill

Article:

368

Can be introduced in either House.

No joint sitting.

---

# Joint Sitting

Article:

108

Presided over by:

Speaker of Lok Sabha.

Purpose:

Resolve deadlock between Houses.

Not applicable for:

- Money Bills
- Constitutional Amendment Bills

---

# Parliamentary Sessions

Three Sessions:

- Budget Session
- Monsoon Session
- Winter Session

Maximum gap:

**Six months**

---

# Quorum

Minimum members required:

**One-tenth (10%)** of total membership.

# Important Supreme Court Case

## Shamsher Singh v. State of Punjab (1974)

Held that:

- President acts on the aid and advice of the Council of Ministers except in limited constitutional situations.

> **AFCAT takeaway:** The President is the constitutional head, while the Prime Minister is the real executive.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### President ≠ Prime Minister

President:
- Constitutional Head

Prime Minister:
- Real Executive

---

### Speaker ≠ Chairman

Speaker:
- Lok Sabha

Chairman:
- Rajya Sabha
- Vice-President

---

### Money Bill ≠ Ordinary Bill

Money Bill:
- Only Lok Sabha
- Article 110

Ordinary Bill:
- Either House

---

### Rajya Sabha ≠ Lok Sabha

Rajya Sabha:
- Permanent House

Lok Sabha:
- Can be dissolved

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The President is the real executive of India.

✅ The **Prime Minister and Council of Ministers** exercise the real executive power.

---

❌ The Money Bill can be introduced in either House.

✅ A **Money Bill can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha**.

---

❌ The Speaker of the Lok Sabha presides over the Rajya Sabha.

✅ The **Vice-President** is the **ex-officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**.

---

❌ Rajya Sabha can reject a Money Bill.

✅ It can **only recommend changes** and must return it within **14 days**.

---

❌ The President is directly elected by the people.

✅ The President is elected **indirectly** by an **Electoral College**.
