# 73rd Constitutional Amendment

Year:

1992

Came into force:

**24 April 1993**

Major Features:

- Constitutional status to Panchayats.
- Three-tier system.
- Five-year tenure.
- Reservation for SCs/STs.
- At least **one-third reservation for women** *(many States now provide higher reservation by State law).*
- State Election Commission.
- State Finance Commission.

---

| Level | Institution |
|---------|------------|
| Village | Gram Panchayat |
| Block/Taluk | Panchayat Samiti |
| District | Zila Parishad |

---

# Gram Sabha

Meaning:

Assembly of all registered voters of a village.

Known as:

Foundation of Panchayati Raj.

Functions:

- Approves local development plans.
- Monitors Panchayat functioning.
- Participates in local governance.

---

# Important Committees

## Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)

Recommended:

- Three-tier Panchayati Raj.

Known as:

**Father of Panchayati Raj.**

---

## Ashok Mehta Committee (1978)

Recommended:

- Stronger district-level administration.
- Greater devolution of powers.

---

# Reservation

Reserved for:

- Scheduled Castes
- Scheduled Tribes
- Women

Chairperson posts are also reserved according to constitutional provisions.

---

# State Election Commission

Conducts elections to:

- Panchayats
- Municipalities

Independent constitutional body.

---

# State Finance Commission

Constituted every:

**5 Years**

Purpose:

Recommend distribution of financial resources between:

- State Government
- Local Bodies

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Gram Sabha ≠ Gram Panchayat

Gram Sabha:
- All registered voters.

Gram Panchayat:
- Elected executive body.

---

### Panchayat Samiti ≠ Zila Parishad

Panchayat Samiti:
- Block Level

Zila Parishad:
- District Level

---

### 73rd Amendment ≠ 74th Amendment

73rd:
- Rural
- Panchayats

74th:
- Urban
- Municipalities

---

### 11th Schedule ≠ 12th Schedule

11th:
- Panchayats
- 29 Subjects

12th:
- Municipalities
- 18 Subjects

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Gram Sabha and Gram Panchayat are the same.

✅ **Gram Sabha** consists of **all registered voters** of the village.
**Gram Panchayat** is the **elected governing body**.

---

❌ The 74th Amendment relates to Panchayati Raj.

✅ The **73rd Amendment** relates to **Panchayati Raj**.
The **74th Amendment** relates to **Municipalities**.

---

❌ The Eleventh Schedule contains Municipal subjects.

✅ The **Eleventh Schedule** contains **Panchayat (Rural) subjects**.
The **Twelfth Schedule** contains **Municipal (Urban) subjects**.

---

❌ The Mayor is the head of a Gram Panchayat.

✅ The **Mayor** heads a **Municipal Corporation**.
A **Gram Panchayat** is headed by its elected **Sarpanch/Pradhan** (designation may vary by State).

---

❌ Panchayat elections are conducted by the Election Commission of India.

✅ They are conducted by the **State Election Commission**.
The **Election Commission of India** conducts elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, and the offices of the President and Vice-President.
