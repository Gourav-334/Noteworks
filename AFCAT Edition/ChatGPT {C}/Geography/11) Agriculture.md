Major Types:

- Food Crops
- Cash Crops
- Plantation Crops
- Horticultural Crops

---

# Agricultural Seasons

India has **three agricultural seasons**.

| Season | Sowing | Harvesting |
|---------|---------|------------|
| Kharif | June–July | September–October |
| Rabi | October–November | March–April |
| Zaid | March–April | June |

---

# Kharif Crops

Also called:

**Monsoon Crops**

Need:

- High temperature
- Heavy rainfall

Major Crops:

- Rice
- Maize
- Cotton
- Jowar
- Bajra
- Soybean
- Groundnut

---

# Rabi Crops

Also called:

**Winter Crops**

Need:

- Cool climate
- Moderate rainfall

Major Crops:

- Wheat
- Gram
- Mustard
- Barley
- Peas

---

# Zaid Crops

Grown:

Between Rabi and Kharif seasons.

Need:

- Warm weather
- Irrigation

Major Crops:

- Watermelon
- Muskmelon
- Cucumber
- Vegetables
- Fodder Crops

# Irrigation

Meaning:

Artificial supply of water to crops.

Importance:

- Increases agricultural productivity.
- Reduces dependence on monsoon.
- Enables multiple cropping.

---

# Major Irrigation Sources

| Source | Importance |
|----------|------------|
| Canals | Northern Plains |
| Wells | Widely used |
| Tube Wells | Punjab, Haryana, UP |
| Tanks | Peninsular India |
| Drip Irrigation | Water-efficient |
| Sprinkler Irrigation | Dry Regions |

---

# Modern Irrigation

## Drip Irrigation

Water supplied:

Directly to roots.

Advantages:

- Saves water.
- Increases yield.

---

## Sprinkler Irrigation

Water sprayed like rainfall.

Suitable for:

- Sandy soils.
- Uneven land.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Kharif ≠ Rabi

Kharif:
- Monsoon
- Rice

Rabi:
- Winter
- Wheat

---

### Tea ≠ Coffee

Tea:
- Assam

Coffee:
- Karnataka

---

### Green Revolution ≠ White Revolution

Green:
- Food Grains
- Wheat

White:
- Milk

---

### Drip ≠ Sprinkler Irrigation

Drip:
- Root Zone

Sprinkler:
- Rain-like Spray

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Wheat is a Kharif crop.

✅ **Wheat is a Rabi crop.**
**Rice** is the major **Kharif crop**.

---

❌ Black soil is ideal for tea cultivation.

✅ **Black soil** is ideal for **cotton**.
**Tea** grows best in **laterite and mountain soils** with high rainfall.

---

❌ The Green Revolution mainly increased rice production across all of India.

✅ The **Green Revolution initially focused on wheat**, especially in **Punjab, Haryana, and Western Uttar Pradesh**.

---

❌ The Father of the White Revolution is M. S. Swaminathan.

✅ **M. S. Swaminathan** is associated with the **Green Revolution**.
**Verghese Kurien** is known as the **Father of the White Revolution**.

---

❌ Drip irrigation wastes more water than sprinkler irrigation.

✅ **Drip irrigation** is one of the **most water-efficient irrigation methods**, delivering water directly to plant roots.
