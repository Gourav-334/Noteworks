# Chapter 5: Mountains, Plateaus & Plains

> **Purpose:** Study the major landforms of the Earth, their formation, characteristics, and important examples.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT commonly asks about **types of mountains, highest peaks, major plateaus, plains, deserts, glaciers, and important world landforms**.

---

# Background

The Earth's surface is divided into three major landforms:

- Mountains
- Plateaus
- Plains

These are formed by:

- Internal Forces (Endogenic)
- External Forces (Exogenic)

---

# Major Landforms

| Landform | Characteristics |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Mountains | High elevation with steep slopes |
| Plateaus | Elevated flat-topped land |
| Plains | Flat or gently sloping lowlands |

---

# Mountains

Mountains are natural elevations of the Earth's surface.

Characteristics:

- High altitude
- Steep slopes
- Cooler climate
- Source of many rivers

---

# Types of Mountains

## Fold Mountains

Formed by:

Compression of tectonic plates.

Characteristics:

- Highest mountains
- Long mountain chains

Examples:

- Himalayas
- Alps
- Andes
- Rockies

---

## Block Mountains

Formed by:

Faulting.

Examples:

- Black Forest (Germany)
- Vosges (France)

---

## Volcanic Mountains

Formed by:

Volcanic eruptions.

Examples:

- Mount Fuji
- Mount Kilimanjaro
- Mount Etna

---

## Residual (Erosional) Mountains

Formed by:

Long-term erosion.

Examples:

- Aravalli Range
- Nilgiri Hills

# Plateaus

Plateaus are elevated flat-topped regions.

Known as:

**Tablelands**

---

# Types of Plateaus

## Intermontane Plateau

Located between mountain ranges.

Example:

- Tibetan Plateau

Known as:

**Roof of the World**

---

## Volcanic Plateau

Formed by lava.

Example:

- Deccan Plateau

---

## Piedmont Plateau

Located at the foot of mountains.

Example:

- Malwa Plateau *(often classified under lava plateaus in Indian geography; conceptually a piedmont region in parts)*

---

## Continental Plateau

Large uplifted regions.

Example:

- Australian Plateau

# Plains

Plains are broad, flat or gently rolling areas.

Characteristics:

- Fertile soil
- Dense population
- Agriculture

---

# Types of Plains

## Structural Plains

Formed by:

Horizontal rock structures.

---

## Erosional Plains

Formed by:

Erosion.

---

## Depositional Plains

Formed by:

Deposition of sediments.

Examples:

- Indo-Gangetic Plain
- Mississippi Plain

---

|--------|----------|
| Indo-Gangetic Plain | India, Pakistan, Bangladesh |
| North China Plain | China |

# Deserts

A desert is an area receiving:

Very low rainfall.

Generally:

Less than **25 cm annually**

---

# Types of Deserts

## Hot Deserts

Examples:

- Sahara
- Thar
- Arabian
- Kalahari

---

## Cold Deserts

Examples:

- Gobi
- Ladakh
- Patagonian Desert *(cold desert characteristics in parts)*

---

# Largest Deserts

| Desert | Type |
|----------|------|
| Antarctica | Cold (Largest overall) |
| Sahara | Largest Hot Desert |
| Thar | India & Pakistan |
| Gobi | Cold Desert |

Found in:

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Everest ≠ K2

Everest:
- Highest Peak
- Himalayas

K2:
- Second Highest
- Karakoram

---

### Sahara ≠ Antarctica

Sahara:
- Largest Hot Desert

Antarctica:
- Largest Desert Overall

---

### Plateau ≠ Plain

Plateau:
- Elevated

Plain:
- Low & Flat

---

### Fold Mountain ≠ Block Mountain

Fold:
- Compression

Block:
- Faulting

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ K2 is located in the Himalayas.

✅ **K2 is located in the Karakoram Range.**

---

❌ Sahara is the largest desert in the world.

✅ **Antarctica** is the **largest desert overall**.
**Sahara** is the **largest hot desert**.

---

❌ Plateaus are formed only by volcanic activity.

✅ Plateaus may be formed by **volcanic activity, uplift, or erosion**.

---

❌ Siachen Glacier is located in the Himalayas.

✅ **Siachen Glacier** lies in the **Karakoram Range**.

---

❌ Mount Everest lies entirely within India.

✅ **Mount Everest** lies on the **Nepal–China (Tibet) border**.
**Kanchenjunga** is the highest peak associated with India.
