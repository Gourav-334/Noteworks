# Earth

Earth is:

- Third planet from the Sun.
- Fifth largest planet.
- Densest planet in the Solar System.
- Only known planet supporting life.

---

# Shape of the Earth

Earth is **not a perfect sphere**.

Actual Shape:

**Oblate Spheroid (Geoid)**

Meaning:

- Slightly flattened at the poles.
- Slightly bulged at the equator.

---

# Movements of the Earth

Earth has two principal motions:

1. Rotation
2. Revolution

---

# Rotation

Meaning:

Earth spins on its own axis.

Direction:

**West → East**

Time Taken:

- **23 hours 56 minutes 4 seconds** (Sidereal Day)
- Approximately **24 hours** (Solar Day)

---

## Effects of Rotation

- Day and Night
- Sunrise in the East
- Sunset in the West
- Difference in local time
- Coriolis Effect *(important for winds and ocean currents)*

---

# Revolution

Meaning:

Earth revolves around the Sun.

Path:

**Elliptical Orbit**

Time Taken:

**365¼ days**

Extra ¼ day:

Results in a **Leap Year** every four years.

---

## Leap Year

Normal Year:

365 days

Leap Year:

366 days

Occurs:

Every 4 years

Exception:

Century years are leap years **only if divisible by 400**.

Examples:

- 2000 ✅ Leap Year
- 1900 ❌ Not a Leap Year
- 2100 ❌ Not a Leap Year

---

# Earth's Axis

Tilt:

**23½° (23.5°)**

Also called:

**Axial Tilt**

Importance:

Responsible for:

- Seasons
- Unequal length of day and night

---

# Day & Night

Caused by:

**Rotation of the Earth**

Since Earth rotates from:

**West → East**

The Sun appears to move:

**East → West**

---

# Seasons

Caused by the combined effect of:

- Revolution
- Axial Tilt (23½°)

> **Seasons are NOT caused by changes in Earth's distance from the Sun.**

---

# Solstices

## Summer Solstice

Date:

**21 June** *(approximately)*

Sun directly overhead at:

**Tropic of Cancer (23½° N)**

Northern Hemisphere:

- Longest Day
- Shortest Night

---

## Winter Solstice

Date:

**22 December** *(approximately)*

Sun directly overhead at:

**Tropic of Capricorn (23½° S)**

Northern Hemisphere:

- Shortest Day
- Longest Night

---

# Equinoxes

During Equinox:

- Day = Night

Sun directly overhead at:

**Equator**

---

## Vernal (Spring) Equinox

Date:

**21 March** *(approximately)*

---

## Autumnal Equinox

Date:

**23 September** *(approximately)*

# Aphelion & Perihelion

## Perihelion

Earth is:

**Closest to the Sun**

Occurs:

Around **3 January**

---

## Aphelion

Earth is:

**Farthest from the Sun**

Occurs:

Around **4 July**

> **These do not cause seasons.**

---

# Moon and Earth

The Moon revolves around Earth in approximately:

**27.3 days (sidereal period)**

Phases of the Moon repeat approximately every:

**29.5 days (synodic month)**

The Moon always shows the same face to Earth because of:

**Synchronous Rotation**

---

# Eclipses

## Solar Eclipse

Occurs when:

**Moon comes between the Sun and Earth.**

Sequence:

Sun → Moon → Earth

Occurs only on:

**New Moon**

---

## Lunar Eclipse

Occurs when:

**Earth comes between the Sun and Moon.**

Sequence:

Sun → Earth → Moon

Occurs only on:

**Full Moon**

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Rotation ≠ Revolution

Rotation:
- Spins on axis
- Day & Night

Revolution:
- Orbits Sun
- Seasons

---

### Equinox ≠ Solstice

Equinox:
- Day = Night
- Sun over Equator

Solstice:
- Longest/Shortest Day
- Sun over Tropics

---

### Solar Eclipse ≠ Lunar Eclipse

Solar:
- Moon between Sun & Earth
- New Moon

Lunar:
- Earth between Sun & Moon
- Full Moon

---

### Perihelion ≠ Aphelion

Perihelion:
- Closest to Sun
- January

Aphelion:
- Farthest from Sun
- July

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Seasons occur because Earth is closer to the Sun in summer.

✅ Seasons are caused by the **tilt of Earth's axis (23½°)** and its **revolution around the Sun**, not by its distance from the Sun.

---

❌ The Earth rotates from East to West.

✅ The Earth rotates from **West to East**, making the Sun appear to rise in the East.

---

❌ The longest day in the Northern Hemisphere occurs on the Equinox.

✅ The **longest day** occurs on the **Summer Solstice (around 21 June)**.

---

❌ A Solar Eclipse occurs during a Full Moon.

✅ A **Solar Eclipse** occurs only during a **New Moon**.
A **Lunar Eclipse** occurs only during a **Full Moon**.

---

❌ Earth is farthest from the Sun during Northern Hemisphere winter.

✅ Earth is **closest** to the Sun at **Perihelion (around 3 January)** and **farthest** at **Aphelion (around 4 July)**.
