# El Niño

Meaning:

Abnormal warming of surface waters in the eastern Pacific Ocean.

Effects:

- Weak Indian Monsoon.
- Drought in some regions.
- Global weather disturbances.

---

# La Niña

Meaning:

Abnormal cooling of eastern Pacific waters.

Effects:

- Stronger Indian Monsoon (often, but not always).
- Increased rainfall in many regions.

---

# Tides

Rise and fall of sea level.

Caused mainly by:

- Moon's Gravity.
- Sun's Gravity.

---

# Types of Tides

## High Tide

Water level rises.

---

## Low Tide

Water level falls.

---

## Spring Tide

Occurs during:

- New Moon
- Full Moon

Highest tidal range.

---

## Neap Tide

Occurs during:

First Quarter & Third (Last) Quarter Moon.

Lowest tidal range.

---

# Waves

Caused mainly by:

Wind blowing over the sea surface.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Gulf ≠ Strait

Gulf:
- Water enclosed by land on three sides.

Strait:
- Narrow water passage connecting two water bodies.

---

### Wave ≠ Tide

Wave:
- Wind

Tide:
- Moon & Sun

---

### El Niño ≠ La Niña

El Niño:
- Warm Pacific
- Weak Indian Monsoon

La Niña:
- Cool Pacific
- Stronger Indian Monsoon

---

### Fringing Reef ≠ Atoll

Fringing:
- Attached to Coast

Atoll:
- Ring-shaped

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The Atlantic Ocean is the largest ocean.

✅ The **Pacific Ocean** is both the **largest and deepest** ocean.

---

❌ El Niño strengthens the Indian Monsoon.

✅ **El Niño** generally **weakens** the Indian Monsoon.
**La Niña** often strengthens it.

---

❌ Spring Tides occur during the first quarter moon.

✅ **Spring Tides** occur during the **New Moon and Full Moon**.
**Neap Tides** occur during the **First and Third (Last) Quarter**.

---

❌ The Great Barrier Reef is an Atoll.

✅ The **Great Barrier Reef** is a **Barrier Reef**.

---

❌ The Strait of Malacca connects the Atlantic and Indian Oceans.

✅ The **Strait of Malacca** connects the **Indian Ocean** and the **Pacific Ocean**.
