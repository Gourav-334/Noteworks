# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ WHO headquarters is in New York.

✅ **WHO** is headquartered in **Geneva, Switzerland**.
**New York** is the headquarters of the **United Nations**.

---

❌ WTO and WHO have the same function.

✅ **WTO** regulates **international trade**.
**WHO** coordinates **global public health**.

---

❌ NATO is a United Nations agency.

✅ **NATO** is a **military alliance** headquartered in **Brussels, Belgium**.
It is **independent of the UN**.

---

❌ World Bank and IMF perform the same role.

✅ **IMF** focuses on **monetary stability and balance-of-payments support**.
**World Bank** provides **development loans and assistance**.

---

❌ SAARC headquarters is located in New Delhi.

✅ **SAARC** is headquartered in **Kathmandu, Nepal**.
