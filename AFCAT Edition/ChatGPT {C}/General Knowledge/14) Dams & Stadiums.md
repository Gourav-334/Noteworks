# Chapter 14: Dams & Stadiums

> **Purpose:** Study India's important dams, rivers, multipurpose river valley projects and major sports stadiums.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT frequently asks **Dam ↔ River ↔ State** and **Stadium ↔ City/Sport**.

---

# Background

Major dams are built for:

- Irrigation
- Hydroelectric Power
- Flood Control
- Drinking Water
- Navigation

Stadium questions are generally direct:

- Stadium → City
- Stadium → Sport

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Bhakra Nangal Dam is built on the Narmada River.

✅ **Bhakra Nangal Dam** is built on the **Sutlej River**.
**Sardar Sarovar Dam** is built on the **Narmada River**.

---

❌ Hirakud Dam is the highest dam in India.

✅ **Hirakud Dam** is the **longest dam in India**.
**Tehri Dam** is the **highest dam in India**.

---

❌ Eden Gardens is located in Mumbai.

✅ **Eden Gardens** is located in **Kolkata**.
**Wankhede Stadium** is located in **Mumbai**.

---

❌ Narendra Modi Stadium is located in New Delhi.

✅ **Narendra Modi Stadium** is located in **Ahmedabad, Gujarat** and is the **largest cricket stadium in the world**.

---

❌ Nagarjuna Sagar Dam is built on the Godavari River.

✅ **Nagarjuna Sagar Dam** is built on the **Krishna River**.
