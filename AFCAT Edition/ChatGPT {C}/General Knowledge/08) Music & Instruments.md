- Folk Music

# Classical Music

India has **two major classical traditions**:

1. Hindustani Classical Music
2. Carnatic Classical Music

---

# Hindustani Classical Music

Region:

North India

Main Forms:

- Dhrupad
- Khayal
- Thumri
- Tappa

---

# Carnatic Classical Music

Region:

South India

Known for:

- Fixed compositions
- Devotional themes

Languages:

- Telugu
- Sanskrit
- Tamil
- Kannada

---

# Hindustani vs Carnatic

| Hindustani | Carnatic |
|-------------|-----------|
| North India | South India |
| More Improvisation | More Structured |
| Khayal, Dhrupad | Kriti |

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Hindustani ≠ Carnatic

Hindustani:
- North India

Carnatic:
- South India

---

### National Anthem ≠ National Song

National Anthem:
- Jana Gana Mana
- Tagore

National Song:
- Vande Mataram
- Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay

---

### Sitar ≠ Sarod

Sitar:
- Ravi Shankar

Sarod:
- Amjad Ali Khan

---

### Tabla ≠ Mridangam

Tabla:
- Hindustani Music

Mridangam:
- Carnatic Music

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Ravi Shankar was a tabla player.

✅ **Pandit Ravi Shankar** was one of the world's greatest **sitar** maestros.

---

❌ Ustad Bismillah Khan was famous for the flute.

✅ **Ustad Bismillah Khan** was internationally renowned for the **shehnai**.

---

❌ Carnatic music originated in North India.

✅ **Carnatic classical music** developed in **South India**.
**Hindustani classical music** is associated with **North India**.

---

❌ Jana Gana Mana was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay.

✅ **Jana Gana Mana** was composed by **Rabindranath Tagore**.
**Vande Mataram** was written by **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**.

---

❌ Tabla is the principal percussion instrument of Carnatic music.

✅ **Tabla** is mainly associated with **Hindustani classical music**.
**Mridangam** is the principal percussion instrument of **Carnatic music**.
