# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The Mahabharata was written by Valmiki.

✅ **Mahabharata** was written by **Ved Vyasa**.
**Valmiki** wrote the **Ramayana**.

---

❌ Bhagavad Gita is an independent epic.

✅ The **Bhagavad Gita** is a **part of the Mahabharata**, consisting of the dialogue between **Lord Krishna and Arjuna**.

---

❌ Samaveda is mainly a collection of hymns.

✅ **Rigveda** is primarily a collection of **hymns**.
**Samaveda** is associated with **music and chants**.

---

❌ Jnanpith Award is presented by Sahitya Akademi.

✅ **Jnanpith Award** is India's **highest literary award**.
**Sahitya Akademi** is a **national literary institution** that presents the Sahitya Akademi Award.

---

❌ Tripitaka is a Jain scripture.

✅ **Tripitaka** is the principal **Buddhist scripture**.
The principal **Jain scriptures** are the **Agamas**.
