- Tamil Nadu

Onam:
- Kerala

---

### Bihu ≠ Baisakhi

- Assam

Baisakhi:
- Punjab

---

### Diwali ≠ Dussehra

- Victory of Good over Evil

---

- Birth of Jesus Christ

- Resurrection of Jesus Christ

---

- Gautama Buddha

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Onam is celebrated in Tamil Nadu.

✅ **Onam** is the famous harvest festival of **Kerala**.
**Pongal** is celebrated in **Tamil Nadu**.

---

❌ Hornbill Festival is celebrated in Assam.

✅ **Hornbill Festival** is celebrated in **Nagaland**.

---

❌ Rath Yatra is associated with Gujarat.

✅ The famous **Jagannath Rath Yatra** is held in **Puri, Odisha**.

---

❌ Kumbh Mela is organized at only one location.

✅ **Kumbh Mela** rotates among **Prayagraj, Haridwar, Nashik and Ujjain**.

---

❌ Baisakhi is celebrated only as a harvest festival.

✅ **Baisakhi** is both a **harvest festival** and commemorates the **formation of the Khalsa (1699)** by **Guru Gobind Singh**.
