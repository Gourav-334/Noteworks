# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Governor-General ≠ Viceroy

Governor-General:
- Before Crown Rule

Viceroy:
- Crown's Representative (1858–1947)

---

### Mountbatten ≠ Rajagopalachari

Mountbatten:
- Last Viceroy
- First Governor-General of Independent India

Rajagopalachari:
- First Indian Governor-General

---

### Osborne Smith ≠ C. D. Deshmukh

Osborne Smith:
- First RBI Governor

C. D. Deshmukh:
- First Indian RBI Governor

---

### Rakesh Sharma ≠ Kalpana Chawla

Rakesh Sharma:
- First Indian in Space

Kalpana Chawla:
- First Indian-born Woman in Space

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Lord Mountbatten was the first Indian Governor-General.

✅ **Lord Mountbatten** was the **first Governor-General of Independent India**, but **C. Rajagopalachari** was the **first Indian Governor-General**.

---

❌ Kalpana Chawla was the first Indian in space.

✅ **Rakesh Sharma** was the **first Indian in space**.
**Kalpana Chawla** was the **first Indian-born woman in space**.

---

❌ Sam Manekshaw was India's first Chief of Defence Staff.

✅ **Sam Manekshaw** was India's **first Field Marshal**.
**General Bipin Rawat** was the **first Chief of Defence Staff (CDS)**.

---

❌ Osborne Smith was the first Indian RBI Governor.

✅ **Osborne Smith** was the **first RBI Governor**.
**C. D. Deshmukh** was the **first Indian RBI Governor**.

---

❌ Vikram Sarabhai was the first Chairman of ISRO.

✅ **Satish Dhawan** was the **first Chairman of ISRO**.
**Vikram Sarabhai** is known as the **Father of the Indian Space Programme**.
