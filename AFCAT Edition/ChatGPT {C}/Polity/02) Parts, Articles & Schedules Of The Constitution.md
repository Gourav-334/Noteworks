# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Article 32 ≠ Article 226

Article 32:
- Supreme Court
- Fundamental Right

Article 226:
- High Court
- Wider Writ Jurisdiction

---

### Seventh Schedule ≠ Eighth Schedule

Seventh:
- Union, State & Concurrent Lists

Eighth:
- Officially Recognized Languages

---

### Fifth Schedule ≠ Sixth Schedule

Fifth:
- Scheduled Areas

Sixth:
- Tribal Areas of Northeast

---

### Article 352 ≠ 356 ≠ 360

352:
- National Emergency

356:
- President's Rule

360:
- Financial Emergency

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ The Seventh Schedule contains Fundamental Rights.

✅ The **Seventh Schedule** contains the **Union, State and Concurrent Lists**.

---

❌ Article 21 deals with Equality before Law.

✅ **Article 14** deals with Equality before Law.
**Article 21** deals with **Right to Life and Personal Liberty**.

---

❌ Article 32 empowers High Courts.

✅ **Article 32** empowers the **Supreme Court** to enforce Fundamental Rights.
**Article 226** relates to the writ jurisdiction of **High Courts**.

---

❌ The Tenth Schedule deals with Panchayats.

✅ The **Tenth Schedule** contains the **Anti-Defection Law**.
The **Eleventh Schedule** deals with **Panchayats**.

---

❌ The 73rd Amendment introduced Municipalities.

✅ The **73rd Amendment** deals with **Panchayati Raj**.
The **74th Amendment** deals with **Municipalities**.
