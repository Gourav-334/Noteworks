# Background

Economic development is measured not only by:

- GDP
- National Income

but also by:

- Education
- Health
- Standard of Living
- Employment

---

# Poverty

Meaning:

A condition where people cannot meet their **basic needs**.

Basic Needs:

- Food
- Clothing
- Shelter
- Education
- Healthcare

---

# Poverty Line

Meaning:

Minimum income or expenditure required to satisfy basic needs.

Purpose:

Used for:

- Measuring poverty.
- Identifying beneficiaries of welfare schemes.

---

# Causes of Poverty

- Unemployment
- Low Income
- Population Growth
- Illiteracy
- Unequal Distribution of Wealth
- Low Agricultural Productivity

---

# Effects of Poverty

- Malnutrition
- Poor Health
- Illiteracy
- Low Productivity
- Social Inequality

---

# Unemployment

Meaning:

A person who is:

- Willing to work.
- Able to work.

but

Cannot find suitable employment.

---

# Types of Unemployment

## Open Unemployment

People have:

No job at all.

---

## Seasonal Unemployment

Occurs during:

Certain seasons.

Common in:

Agriculture.

---

## Disguised Unemployment

More workers employed than actually needed.

Common in:

Agriculture.

Example:

Five people doing work that only three people are needed for.

---

## Structural Unemployment

Occurs due to:

Changes in economy or technology.

Workers' skills do not match available jobs.

---

## Frictional Unemployment

Temporary unemployment while:

Changing jobs.

---

## Educated Unemployment

Educated persons unable to find suitable employment.

Common in:

Developing countries.

---

# Employment vs Unemployment

| Employment | Unemployment |
|-------------|--------------|
| Working | Not Working |
| Income | No Regular Income |

---

# Labour Force

Includes:

People who are:

- Working
- Actively seeking work

Excludes:

- Children
- Retired Persons
- Those not seeking employment

---

# Workforce

People who are:

Actually employed.

---

# Human Development

Meaning:

Expansion of people's:

- Choices
- Opportunities
- Capabilities

Introduced by:

**UNDP**

---

# Human Development Index (HDI)

Prepared by:

**United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)**

Measures:

Three Dimensions:

- Health
- Education
- Standard of Living

---

# HDI Indicators

| Dimension | Indicator |
|------------|-----------|
| Health | Life Expectancy |
| Education | Years of Schooling |
| Standard of Living | Gross National Income (GNI) per Capita |

---

# Human Development Report

Published by:

**UNDP**

Frequency:

Annually.

---

# Sustainable Development

Meaning:

Development that satisfies present needs

without affecting

future generations.

---

# Inclusive Growth

# Social Sector

Includes:

- Education
- Healthcare

# Literacy Rate

Meaning:

Percentage of people aged **7 years and above**

who can:

Read and write with understanding.

---

# Demographic Dividend

Meaning:

Economic advantage when:

Working-age population is larger than dependent population.

India is currently experiencing a demographic dividend.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### GDP ≠ HDI

GDP:
- Economic Output

HDI:
- Quality of Life

---

### Labour Force ≠ Workforce

Labour Force:
- Working + Seeking Work

Workforce:
- Only Working

---

### Seasonal ≠ Disguised Unemployment

Seasonal:
- Certain Times of Year

Disguised:
- Extra Workers

---

### MDGs ≠ SDGs

MDGs:
- 8 Goals

SDGs:
- 17 Goals

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ HDI is prepared by the World Bank.

✅ **HDI** is prepared by the **United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)**.

---

❌ GDP and HDI measure the same thing.

✅ **GDP** measures **economic production**.
**HDI** measures **overall human development** using health, education and income.

---

❌ Seasonal unemployment mainly occurs in industries.

✅ **Seasonal unemployment** is most commonly associated with **agriculture**.

---

❌ SDGs contain 8 goals.

✅ The **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** contain **17 goals**.
The **Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)** had **8 goals**.

---

❌ Labour Force and Workforce are identical.

✅ **Labour Force** includes both **employed people and those actively seeking work**.
**Workforce** includes **only employed people**.
