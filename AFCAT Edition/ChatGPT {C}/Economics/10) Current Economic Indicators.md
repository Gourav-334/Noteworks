# Chapter 10: Current Economic Indicators

> **Purpose:** Study the major economic indicators used to measure inflation, industrial growth, foreign trade, investment and the overall health of the Indian economy.

> **Exam Tip:** AFCAT frequently asks about **CPI, WPI, IIP, Balance of Payments (BoP), Foreign Exchange Reserves, FDI, FPI and Exchange Rate**.

---

# Background

Economic indicators help governments, businesses and investors understand:

- Economic Growth
- Inflation
- Industrial Performance
- Trade
- Employment
- Investment

---

# Consumer Price Index (CPI)

Measures:

**Retail Inflation**

Tracks:

Prices paid by consumers.

Used by:

**Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**

Purpose:

Inflation targeting.

---

# Wholesale Price Index (WPI)

Measures:

Wholesale prices.

Reflects:

Producer-level inflation.

Published by:

Government of India.

---

# CPI vs WPI

| CPI | WPI |
|------|-----|
| Retail Prices | Wholesale Prices |
| Consumer Level | Producer Level |
| Used by RBI | Economic Analysis |

---

# Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

Measures:

Growth of industrial production.

Published by:

**National Statistics Office (NSO)**

Three Main Sectors:

- Mining
- Manufacturing
- Electricity

Importance:

Indicator of industrial growth.

---

# Purchasing Managers' Index (PMI)

Measures:

Business activity.

Types:

- Manufacturing PMI
- Services PMI

Interpretation:

- Above **50** → Expansion
- Below **50** → Contraction

---

# Balance of Payments (BoP)

Meaning:

Record of all economic transactions between:

Residents of India

and

Rest of the World.

---

# Components of BoP

## Current Account

Includes:

- Exports
- Imports
- Services
- Income
- Transfers

---

## Capital Account

Includes:

- Foreign Investment
- External Borrowing
- Capital Transfers

---

# Balance of Trade (BoT)

Meaning:

Difference between:

Exports

and

Imports

of **Goods Only**.

Formula:

**BoT = Exports − Imports**

---

# BoP vs BoT

| BoP | BoT |
|------|-----|
| Goods + Services + Capital | Goods Only |
| Wider Concept | Narrow Concept |

---

# Trade Deficit

Occurs when:

Imports > Exports

---

# Trade Surplus

Occurs when:

Exports > Imports

---

# Foreign Exchange Reserves (Forex Reserves)

Meaning:

Foreign currencies and reserve assets held by the **RBI**.

Purpose:

- Pay for imports.
- Stabilize the Rupee.
- Meet international obligations.

Components:

- Foreign Currency Assets (largest component)
- Gold
- SDRs
- Reserve Tranche Position (IMF)

---

# Exchange Rate

Meaning:

Value of one currency in terms of another.

Example:

₹ against US Dollar.

---

# Currency Appreciation

Meaning:

Domestic currency becomes stronger.

Effects:

- Imports become cheaper.
- Exports become relatively costlier.

---

# Currency Depreciation

Meaning:

Domestic currency becomes weaker.

Effects:

- Exports become relatively cheaper.
- Imports become more expensive.

---

# Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)

Meaning:

Long-term investment by a foreign company in another country.

Examples:

- Building factories.
- Setting up industries.

Characteristics:

- Long-term.
- Greater control.

---

# Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI)

Meaning:

Investment in:

- Shares
- Bonds
- Financial Securities

Characteristics:

- Short-term.
- No management control.
- Can move quickly between countries.

---

# FDI vs FPI

| FDI | FPI |
|------|-----|
| Long-Term | Short-Term |
| Physical Investment | Financial Investment |
| Management Control | No Control |

---

# Gross Value Added (GVA)

Meaning:

Measures:

Value added by different sectors of the economy.

Formula:

Output

−

Intermediate Consumption

Importance:

Used to estimate sector-wise economic performance.

---

# Ease of Doing Business

Purpose:

Improve:

- Investment
- Entrepreneurship
- Business Environment

---

# Ease of Living Index

Measures:

Quality of urban life.

---

# Credit Rating

Purpose:

Measures ability to repay debt.

Major Global Agencies:

- Moody's
- Standard & Poor's (S&P)
- Fitch Ratings

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ CPI measures wholesale inflation.

✅ **CPI** measures **retail inflation**.
**WPI** measures **wholesale inflation**.

---

❌ Balance of Trade includes services and foreign investments.

✅ **Balance of Trade (BoT)** includes **only exports and imports of goods**.
**Balance of Payments (BoP)** includes goods, services and capital transactions.

---

❌ FDI and FPI are the same type of investment.

✅ **FDI** is **long-term investment** involving business ownership or control.
**FPI** is investment in **financial securities** without management control.

---

❌ A stronger Rupee makes exports cheaper.

✅ A **stronger (appreciating) Rupee** generally makes **exports more expensive** and **imports cheaper**.

---

❌ IIP measures inflation.

✅ **IIP (Index of Industrial Production)** measures **industrial output**, not inflation.
