# LPG Reforms (1991)

Introduced due to:

Economic Crisis.

LPG stands for:

- Liberalisation
- Privatisation
- Globalisation

---

## Liberalisation

Meaning:

Reduction of Government controls.

Effects:

- Easier business.
- Greater competition.

---

## Privatisation

Meaning:

Greater role of the private sector.

Examples:

- Disinvestment.
- Private participation.

---

## Globalisation

Meaning:

Integration with the world economy.

Effects:

- Foreign Investment.
- International Trade.

Growth:
- GDP Increase

- GDP + Living Standards

---

### Planning Commission ≠ NITI Aayog

- Five-Year Plans

NITI Aayog:
- Policy Advisory Body

---

### Public Sector ≠ Private Sector

Public:
- Government

Private:
- Individuals/Companies

---

### Liberalisation ≠ Privatisation

Liberalisation:
- Fewer Government Restrictions

Privatisation:
- Greater Private Ownership/Participation

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ NITI Aayog prepares Five-Year Plans.

✅ **NITI Aayog** replaced the **Planning Commission** in **2015** and functions as a **policy think tank**. It does **not** prepare Five-Year Plans.

---

❌ Liberalisation means transferring government companies to private ownership.

✅ **Liberalisation** means **reducing government controls and regulations**.
**Privatisation** refers to increasing **private sector ownership or participation**.

---

❌ Economic growth and economic development are identical.

✅ **Economic Growth** mainly refers to an increase in **Real GDP**.
**Economic Development** includes growth along with improvements in **education, health and living standards**.

---

❌ Agriculture belongs to the Secondary Sector.

✅ Agriculture is part of the **Primary Sector**.
Manufacturing belongs to the **Secondary Sector**.

---

❌ India has a purely capitalist economy.

✅ India follows a **Mixed Economy**, combining both **public** and **private** sectors.
