- Supplies raw materials to industries.

---

# Minimum Support Price (MSP)

Meaning:

Minimum price announced by the Government for certain crops.

Purpose:

Protect farmers from sudden fall in market prices.

Announced by:

Government of India.

Recommended by:

**Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)**

---

# Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP)

Established:

**1965** *(originally Agricultural Prices Commission)*

Purpose:

Recommend:

- MSP for major crops.

---

# Procurement

Meaning:

Government purchases crops from farmers at MSP.

Main Agency:

**Food Corporation of India (FCI)**

---

# Food Corporation of India (FCI)

Established:

**1965**

Headquarters:

**New Delhi**

Functions:

- Procurement of food grains.
- Storage.
- Distribution.
- Maintain Buffer Stock.

---

# Buffer Stock

Meaning:

Reserve stock of food grains maintained by FCI.

Purpose:

- Food Security.
- Price Stabilization.
- Emergency Relief.

---

# Public Distribution System (PDS)

Purpose:

Supply essential food items at subsidized prices.

Major Commodities:

- Rice
- Wheat
- Sugar
- Kerosene *(historically; coverage has changed over time)*

Beneficiaries:

Eligible households through Fair Price Shops.

---

# National Food Security Act (NFSA)

Enacted:

**2013**

Objective:

Provide subsidized food grains to eligible households.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### MSP ≠ Market Price

MSP:
- Government-Assured Minimum Price

Market Price:
- Determined by Demand & Supply

---

### FCI ≠ PDS

FCI:
- Procurement & Storage

PDS:
- Distribution to Consumers

---

### Green Revolution ≠ White Revolution

Green:
- Wheat/Food Grains

White:
- Milk

---

### MSME ≠ SEZ

MSME:
- Enterprise Classification

SEZ:
- Special Export-Oriented Zone

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ MSP is decided by the Food Corporation of India.

✅ **MSP** is **recommended by the CACP** and **announced by the Government of India**.
The **FCI** mainly procures and stores food grains.

---

❌ PDS purchases food grains directly from farmers.

✅ The **FCI** procures food grains.
The **PDS** distributes them to eligible beneficiaries through Fair Price Shops.

---

❌ The Green Revolution focused mainly on rice across all regions.

✅ The **Green Revolution** initially achieved its greatest success with **wheat**, especially in northwestern India.

---

❌ MSME refers only to manufacturing industries.

✅ **MSMEs** operate in both the **manufacturing** and **services** sectors.

---

❌ SEZs are created mainly for agriculture.

✅ **Special Economic Zones (SEZs)** are established to **promote exports, investment, and industrial development**.
