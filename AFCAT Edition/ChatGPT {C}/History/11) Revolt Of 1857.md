# Chapter 11: Revolt of 1857

> **Also Known As:**
> - First War of Indian Independence (by many Indian historians)
> - Sepoy Mutiny (British historians)
> - Revolt of 1857
>
> **Nature:** Military uprising that developed into a widespread rebellion in northern and central India.

---

# Timeline

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1848–1856 | Doctrine of Lapse implemented |

---

# Causes of the Revolt

## Political Causes

- Doctrine of Lapse
- Annexation of Awadh (1856)
- Removal of traditional rulers
- Expansionist British policies

---

## Economic Causes

- Heavy land revenue
- Exploitation of peasants
- Decline of handicrafts
- Economic hardships for artisans

---

## Military Causes

- Discrimination against Indian soldiers
- Lower salaries
- Fewer promotion opportunities
- Overseas service concerns

---

## Religious & Social Causes

- Fear of forced conversion
- Social reform measures misunderstood
- Suspicion of British intentions

---

## Immediate Cause

### Enfield Rifle

New cartridges were rumored to be greased with:

- Cow fat
- Pig fat

Cartridges had to be bitten open.

This offended:

- Hindus
- Muslims

---

# Beginning of the Revolt

## Mangal Pandey

Date:

**29 March 1857**

Place:

**Barrackpore**

Importance:

- First soldier to openly revolt.
- Executed on **8 April 1857**.

---

## Meerut

Date:

**10 May 1857**

Importance:

- Beginning of the large-scale uprising.

Rebels marched to:

**Delhi**

# Important Leaders

## Bahadur Shah II (Zafar)

- Last Mughal Emperor.
- Declared symbolic leader of the revolt.
- Later exiled to **Rangoon (Yangon)**.

---

## Nana Sahib

- Adopted son of Peshwa Baji Rao II.
- Led the revolt at Kanpur.

Advisor:

**Azimullah Khan**

---

## Rani Lakshmibai

Queen of:

**Jhansi**

Known for:

- Extraordinary bravery.
- Fought the British until her death in **1858**.

---

## Tantia Tope

Known for:

- Brilliant guerrilla warfare.
- Close associate of Nana Sahib.

Executed in:

**1859**

---

## Begum Hazrat Mahal

Led the revolt in:

**Lucknow**

Supported her son:

**Birjis Qadr**

---

## Kunwar Singh

Leader of:

**Jagdishpur (Bihar)**

Age during revolt:

Around **80 years**

---

# Areas That Did Not Join

Major regions remaining largely loyal or neutral:

- Punjab
- Bengal (most parts)
- Hyderabad
- Kashmir
- Many princely states

Many Sikh, Gurkha and Pathan soldiers fought for the British.

---

# Reasons for Failure

- No central leadership.
- Poor coordination.
- Limited geographical spread.
- Lack of modern weapons.
- Many princely states remained loyal to the British.
- British had superior communication (railways & telegraph).

---

# Results of the Revolt

## Political

- East India Company abolished.
- Crown Rule established.
- Government of India Act (1858).

---

## Administrative

- Office of Governor-General continued.
- Governor-General also became **Viceroy**.

First Viceroy:

**Lord Canning**

---

## Military

- Reorganization of the army.
- Increased proportion of European soldiers.
- Policy of "Divide and Rule" strengthened.

---

## Social

- British became more cautious regarding social reforms.
- Greater emphasis on alliances with princely states.

---

# Government of India Act (1858)

Main Provisions:

- Company Rule abolished.
- India came under the British Crown.
- Secretary of State for India created.
- Viceroy became representative of the British Crown.

---

# Queen Victoria's Proclamation (1858)

Promised:

- Religious tolerance.
- Equal treatment before law (in principle).
- Protection of princely states.
- Non-interference in religious matters.
