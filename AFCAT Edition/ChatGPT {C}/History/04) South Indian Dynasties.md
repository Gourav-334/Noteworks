# Pallava Dynasty

## Capital

**Kanchipuram**

---

## Important Rulers

### Mahendravarman I

- Patron of art
- Began rock-cut architecture

---

### Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla)

- Defeated Chalukya ruler Pulakeshin II
- Founded Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram) as an important port

---

## Contributions

- Rock-cut temples
- Shore Temple
- Pancha Rathas

UNESCO Site:

**Mahabalipuram**

- Kanchipuram
- Shore Temple
- Mahabalipuram

Chola:
- Thanjavur
- Brihadeeswara Temple
- Strong Navy

---

- Aihole

---

- Gangaikonda Cholapuram

---

- Vanji

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Brihadeeswara Temple was built by the Pallavas.

✅ It was built by **Rajaraja I of the Cholas**.

---

❌ Kailasa Temple is located at Mahabalipuram.

✅ It is located at **Ellora**, Maharashtra.

---

❌ Pulakeshin II defeated Samudragupta.

✅ He defeated **Harshavardhana** near the **Narmada River**.

---

❌ Sangam Literature was written in Sanskrit.

✅ It was primarily written in **Tamil**.

---

❌ Shore Temple was built by the Cholas.

✅ It was built during the **Pallava** period at **Mahabalipuram**.
