# Chapter 1: Indus Valley Civilization (c. 3300–1300 BCE)

> **Also Known As:** Harappan Civilization  
> **Type:** Bronze Age Urban Civilization  
> **Period of Maturity:** c. 2600–1900 BCE

---

# Timeline

| Period | Years |
|---------|--------|
| Early Harappan | c. 3300–2600 BCE |
| Mature Harappan | c. 2600–1900 BCE |
| Late Harappan | c. 1900–1300 BCE |

---

# Discovery

- First discovered site: **Harappa**
- Discovered by **Daya Ram Sahni (1921)**
- Mohenjo-daro discovered by **R. D. Banerji (1922)**
- Civilization identified by **Sir John Marshall**

---

| Site | Present Location | Important Facts |

# Town Planning

## Features

- Grid pattern roads
- Well-planned drainage system
- Standardized baked bricks
- Covered drains
- Underground sewage
- Public wells
- Citadel and Lower Town
- Houses opened into lanes instead of main roads

### City Division

**Citadel**
- Administrative area
- Public buildings
- Granaries
- Great Bath

**Lower Town**
- Residential area
- Markets
- Workshops

### Lothal ≠ Dholavira

- Lothal → Dockyard
- Dholavira → Reservoirs & Signboard

---

### Kalibangan ≠ Banawali

- Banawali → Barley cultivation

---

### Dancing Girl ≠ Priest King

- Priest King → Steatite
