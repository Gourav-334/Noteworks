# Important Constitutional Acts

## Regulating Act (1773)

- First major law to regulate East India Company administration.
- Created the post of **Governor-General of Bengal**.
- Established the **Supreme Court at Calcutta (1774)**.

---

## Pitt's India Act (1784)

- Increased British Government control over the East India Company.
- Introduced **Board of Control**.

---

## Charter Act (1833)

- Made the **Governor-General of Bengal** the **Governor-General of India**.
- Ended Company's commercial activities.
- Centralized administration.

---

## Government of India Act (1858)

- Rule transferred from East India Company to the British Crown.
- Office of **Viceroy** created.

---

## Government of India Act (1919)

Also called:

**Montagu–Chelmsford Reforms**

Main Feature:

- Dyarchy in Provinces

---

## Government of India Act (1935)

Main Features:

- Provincial Autonomy
- Federal structure (not fully implemented)
- Basis for many provisions of the present Indian Constitution

# Major Governor-Generals & Viceroys

---

# Warren Hastings (1774–1785)

**First Governor-General of Bengal**

Achievements:

- Reorganized administration.
- Strengthened revenue collection.
- Established civil and criminal courts.

Associated with:

- Regulating Act (1773)
- Pitt's India Act (1784)

---

# Lord Cornwallis (1786–1793)

Known as:

**Father of Civil Services in India**

Achievements:

- Introduced **Permanent Settlement (1793)**.
- Cornwallis Code.
- Judicial reforms.

---

## Permanent Settlement

Introduced in:

- Bengal
- Bihar
- Orissa

Land revenue collected through:

**Zamindars**

---

# Lord Wellesley (1798–1805)

Introduced:

**Subsidiary Alliance**

Main Purpose:

- Expand British political control.

Indian rulers accepted:

- British troops.
- British Resident.
- No independent foreign policy.

---

# Lord Hastings (1813–1823)

Achievements:

- Defeat of the Marathas.
- End of Pindari menace.
- Expansion of British territories.

---

# Lord William Bentinck (1828–1835)

**First Governor-General of India**

Achievements:

- Abolished **Sati (1829)**.
- Suppressed Thuggee.
- Promoted English education.

---

## English Education

Based on:

**Macaulay's Minute (1835)**

Supported by:

**Thomas Babington Macaulay**

---

# Lord Dalhousie (1848–1856)

Introduced:

**Doctrine of Lapse**

---

## Doctrine of Lapse

States without a natural male heir could be annexed.

Important annexations:

- Satara
- Jhansi
- Nagpur

---

## Other Achievements

- First Railway (Bombay–Thane, 1853)
- Electric Telegraph
- Modern Postal System

---

# Lord Canning (1856–1862)

**Last Governor-General of India**

**First Viceroy of India**

Achievements:

- Handled Revolt of 1857.
- Government of India Act (1858).

---

# Lord Mayo (1869–1872)

Achievements:

- Mayo College (Ajmer)
- Financial decentralization

---

# Lord Lytton (1876–1880)

Associated with:

- Vernacular Press Act (1878)
- Arms Act (1878)
- Second Anglo-Afghan War

---

# Lord Ripon (1880–1884)

Known as:

**Father of Local Self-Government in India**

Achievements:

- Repealed Vernacular Press Act.
- Local Self-Government reforms.
- Hunter Commission (Education).

---

# Lord Dufferin (1884–1888)

Most Important Event:

**Indian National Congress founded (1885)**

---

# Lord Curzon (1899–1905)

Achievements:

- Partition of Bengal (1905)
- Indian Universities Act (1904)
- Archaeological Survey reforms

---

# Partition of Bengal

Year:

1905

Reason (Official):

Administrative convenience.

Actual Effect:

Rise of the Swadeshi Movement.

---

# Lord Hardinge II (1910–1916)

Achievements:

- Capital shifted:
  - Calcutta → Delhi (1911)
- Bengal Partition annulled (1911)

---

# Lord Chelmsford (1916–1921)

Associated with:

- Government of India Act (1919)
- Montagu–Chelmsford Reforms
- Rowlatt Act
- Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

---

# Lord Irwin (1926–1931)

Associated with:

- Gandhi-Irwin Pact
- First Round Table Conference

---

# Lord Willingdon (1931–1936)

Associated with:

- Second Round Table Conference
- Civil Disobedience Movement

---

# Lord Linlithgow (1936–1943)

Longest-serving Viceroy.

Associated with:

- Government of India Act (1935)
- Second World War
- Quit India Movement

---

# Lord Wavell (1943–1947)

Associated with:

- Wavell Plan

# Lord Mountbatten (1947–1948)

- Last Viceroy of India.

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Warren Hastings was the first Governor-General of India.

✅ He was the **first Governor-General of Bengal**.

---

❌ Lord Canning was the last Viceroy.

✅ **Lord Mountbatten** was the **last Viceroy**, while **Lord Canning** was the **first Viceroy**.

---

❌ Doctrine of Lapse was introduced by Wellesley.

✅ It was introduced by **Lord Dalhousie**.

---

❌ Permanent Settlement was introduced by Dalhousie.

✅ It was introduced by **Lord Cornwallis** in **1793**.

---

❌ The capital shifted to Delhi during Curzon's tenure.

✅ The capital shifted in **1911** during **Lord Hardinge II**.
