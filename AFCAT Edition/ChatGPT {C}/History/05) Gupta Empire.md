# Chapter 5: Gupta Empire (c. 320–550 CE)

> **Founder:** Chandragupta I
>
> **Capital:** Pataliputra
>
> **Known As:** The Golden Age of Ancient India
>
> **Period of Greatest Prosperity:** 4th–5th Century CE

---

# Background

- Founded by **Chandragupta I** in northern India.
- The Gupta rulers united much of northern India after the decline of the Kushanas.
- Their reign witnessed remarkable progress in:
  - Literature
  - Science
  - Mathematics
  - Astronomy
  - Art
  - Architecture

---

# Important Rulers

## Chandragupta I (c. 320–335 CE)

- Founder of the Gupta Empire.
- Married **Kumaradevi**, a Lichchhavi princess.
- Assumed the title **Maharajadhiraja**.
- Expanded the kingdom through both conquest and marriage alliances.

---

## Samudragupta (c. 335–375 CE)

Known as:

**"Napoleon of India"** (V. A. Smith)

Achievements:

- Conquered most of northern India.
- Conducted successful southern campaigns.
- Allowed many defeated southern rulers to continue as tributaries.

Main source of information:

**Allahabad Pillar Inscription (Prayag Prashasti)**

Written by:

**Harisena**

---

## Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)

Achievements:

- Defeated the Western Kshatrapas (Shakas).
- Expanded trade with western ports.
- Ujjain became an important commercial centre.

Chinese traveller:

**Faxian** visited India during his reign.

---

## Kumaragupta I

Achievements:

- Maintained stability.
- Traditionally credited with the establishment of **Nalanda University**.

---

## Skandagupta

Achievements:

- Successfully repelled the Hun invasions.
- Last powerful Gupta ruler.

---

# Administration

Capital:

**Pataliputra**

Features:

- Strong central monarchy.
- Provinces governed by appointed officials.
- Increasing land grants during the later period.
- Local administration became more important over time.

---

# Economy

Main occupation:

- Agriculture

Other features:

- Flourishing trade
- Gold coinage (Dinara)
- Textile industry
- Silk trade
- Internal and foreign trade

---

# Society

Features:

- Varna system continued.
- Urban centres flourished.
- Prosperity increased.
- Guilds (Shrenis) played an important economic role.

---

# Religion

Royal patronage mainly to:

- Vaishnavism

However:

- Buddhism
- Jainism
- Shaivism

also flourished.

The Gupta rulers generally followed a policy of religious tolerance.

---

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Chandragupta I ≠ Chandragupta Maurya

Chandragupta Maurya:
- Maurya Empire
- Chanakya
- Megasthenes

Chandragupta I:
- Gupta Empire
- Kumaradevi
- Beginning of Golden Age

---

### Faxian ≠ Hiuen Tsang ≠ Megasthenes

Megasthenes:
- Maurya Empire

Faxian:
- Gupta Empire

Hiuen Tsang:
- Harshavardhana

---

### Harisena ≠ Kalidasa

Harisena:
- Wrote Prayag Prashasti

Kalidasa:
- Sanskrit poet and dramatist

---

### Aryabhata ≠ Brahmagupta

Aryabhata:
- Aryabhatiya
- Earth's rotation

Brahmagupta:
- Zero
- Algebra
