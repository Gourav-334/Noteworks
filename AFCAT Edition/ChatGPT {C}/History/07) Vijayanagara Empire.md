# Chapter 7: Vijayanagara Empire (1336–1646 CE)

> **Founded By:** Harihara I and Bukka Raya I
>
> **Capital:** Vijayanagara (Hampi)
>
> **Founded With the Guidance Of:** Vidyaranya

# Foundation

Founders:

- Harihara I
- Bukka Raya I

Spiritual guide:

**Vidyaranya**

Capital:

**Vijayanagara (Hampi)**

Purpose:

- Resist expansion of the Delhi Sultanate and later the Bahmani Sultanate.
- Protect and revive Hindu political power in South India.

---

# Important Rulers

## Harihara I

- Co-founder of the empire.
- Established control over the Tungabhadra region.

---

## Bukka Raya I

- Expanded the empire considerably.
- Encouraged trade and agriculture.

---

## Deva Raya II

Known for:

- Military strength
- Efficient administration
- Patronage of literature

Persian traveller **Abdur Razzaq** visited during his reign.

---

## Krishnadevaraya (1509–1529)

Greatest ruler of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Achievements:

- Defeated the Bahmani successor states.
- Won the **Battle of Raichur (1520)**.
- Expanded the empire to its greatest extent.
- Patron of literature, art and architecture.

---

# Administration

Features:

- Strong centralized monarchy.
- Efficient provincial administration.
- Revenue mainly from land.
- Local self-government continued.

---

## Nayankara System

Land grants were given to military chiefs called:

**Nayakas**

Responsibilities:

- Maintain troops.
- Collect revenue.
- Maintain law and order.

---

# Battle of Raichur (1520)

Between:

- Krishnadevaraya
- Adil Shah of Bijapur

Result:

- Victory of Vijayanagara Empire.

---

# Battle of Talikota (1565)

Also called:

**Battle of Rakshasa-Tangadi**

Between:

- Vijayanagara Empire
- Deccan Sultanates

Result:

- Decisive defeat of Vijayanagara.
- Hampi was destroyed and plundered.
- Beginning of the empire's decline.

# Easy-to-Confuse Facts

### Battle of Raichur ≠ Battle of Talikota

Raichur (1520):
- Krishnadevaraya's victory.

Talikota (1565):
- Vijayanagara's defeat.

---

### Domingo Paes ≠ Abdur Razzaq

Domingo Paes:
- Portuguese
- Krishnadevaraya

- Persian
- Deva Raya II

---

- Vittala Temple
- Hampi

- Thanjavur

---

- Founders

- Greatest ruler

# Common AFCAT Traps

❌ Vijayanagara was founded by Krishnadevaraya.

✅ It was founded by **Harihara I and Bukka Raya I**.

---

❌ Stone Chariot is located at Konark.

✅ The famous **Stone Chariot** is at the **Vittala Temple, Hampi**.
> *(Konark Sun Temple also has a chariot-like design, but the freestanding stone chariot asked in AFCAT refers to Hampi.)*

---

❌ Battle of Talikota was won by Krishnadevaraya.

✅ Krishnadevaraya won the **Battle of Raichur (1520)**.
The **Battle of Talikota (1565)** occurred after his reign and led to Vijayanagara's decline.

---

❌ Amuktamalyada was written by Allasani Peddana.

✅ It was written by **Krishnadevaraya**.

---

❌ Hampi and Vijayanagara are different cities.

✅ **Hampi is the site of the capital city of Vijayanagara**, now a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
