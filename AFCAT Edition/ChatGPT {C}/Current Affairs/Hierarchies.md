## **Topic - 1: Indian Army**

### <u>Officers & Roles</u>

| Rank                   | Typical Command/Role                             |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lieutenant**         | Platoon / troop-level leadership                 |
| **Captain**            | Company second-in-command or equivalent          |
| **Major**              | Company commander / staff appointment            |
| **Lieutenant Colonel** | Battalion commander                              |
| **Colonel**            | Brigade staff/command and senior appointments    |
| **Brigadier**          | Brigade commander                                |
| **Major General**      | Division commander                               |
| **Lieutenant General** | Corps commander / senior Army HQ appointment     |
| **General**            | Chief of the Army Staff (COAS)                   |
| **Field Marshal**      | Five-star ceremonial rank; exceptionally awarded |


### <u>JCOs</u>

- *Naib Subedar* $\rightarrow$ *Subedar* $\rightarrow$ *Subedar Major*.
- Linkage between officers & enlisted personnel at company/battalion level.


### <u>NCOs</u>

- *Sepoy*, *Lance Naik* $\rightarrow$ *Naik* $\rightarrow$ *Havildar*.

## **Topic - 2: Indian Air Force**

### <u>History</u>

|   Year    | Description                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :-------: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  1932-45  | First flight in 1933 with 6 RAF trained Indian officers & aircrafts like *Westland Wapiti*, *Spitfire*, etc. Got **Royal** prefix in 1945 after 1944 *Burma campaign*.        |
|  1947-50  | Supported Army by airlifting troops during *First Kashmir War*, and in 1950 the **Royal** prefix was dropped.                                                                 |
|  1950-62  | Inducted fighter jets like *Vampire*, *Hunter*, *MiG-21*, *Electric Canberra*, etc. Post Sino-Indian war exposed air limitations of IAF.                                      |
|   1965    | IAF fought under leadership of *Air Chief Marshal Arjan Singh*, who became the first Air Chief & Marshal Of IAF.                                                              |
|   1971    | During *Bangladesh Liberation War*, IAF flew more than $4,000$ sorties with included aircrafts like *Mig-21*, *Gnat*, *Hunter*, *Su-7*, *Canberra*, *An-12*, etc.             |
| 1980-1990 | IAF inducted modern aircrafts which it is still using today, including *Su-30* & *Jaguar*. Also its helicopters & transport aircrafts became crucial in *Operation Meghdoot*. |
|   1999    | During *Kargil War*, IAF carried out *Operation Safed Sagar* where *Mirage-2000* became notable for precision strikes.                                                        |

### <u>Rescue & Humanitarian Operations</u>

#### Operation Rahat:

- Uttarakhand, 2013
- Used *Mi-17V5*, *ALH*, *Cheetah*, etc.
- More than $1,540$ sorties.
- $13,052$ people evacuated.
- $216$ tons of relief material.

#### Operation Megh Rahat:

- J&K floods, 2014
- $70+$ aircrafts participated.
- $96,000$ people evacuated.
- $3,500$ tons of relief material.

#### Wayanad Landslides:

- Kerela, 2024
- Aircrafts used are *C-17*, *C-130J*, *An-32*, *Mi-17*, and *ALH*.
- $200+$ people rescued.
- $53$ tons of rescue equipment.

#### Jammu & Punjab Floods:

- Jammu & Punjab, 2025
- Aircrafts used are *Mi-17*, *Chinook*, and *C-130J*.

#### Myanmar Earthquakes:

- Aircrafts used are *C-130J* & *C-17* aircraft.
- $177$ tons of relief materials.

#### International Evacuation:

- *C-17* evacuated Indians from *Trailand*, just rescued from *Myanmar*.
- *C-17* evacuated Indians during *Iran-Israel* conflict.
