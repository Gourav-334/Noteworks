## **Topic - 1: String Of Pearls**

### <u>Introduction</u>

- *String Of Pearls* is a Chinese strategy to surround India using its naval power.
- Name comes from a plant which looks like pearls attached with string.
- *Pearls* here are bases, and *string* is network.
- To make it true, it created networks with other nations' ports.


### <u>Strategy</u>

- China started its petroleum trade with other nations, but gradually shifted to establishing its military power there.

## **Topic - 2: Necklace Of Diamond**

### <u>Introduction</u>

- India's counter-strategy to China's *String Of Pearls*.
- Some nations are shared between China & India because the alliance treaties are signed as trade partnership, not military cooperation.


### <u>Strategy</u>

- Formation of *Quad* with Australia, Japan, and USA.
- *Quad* is a naval alliance to avoid Chinese dominance in *Indian Ocean*.
