It is also the first city where I noticed HAL, ISRO, Air Force, BEL, and many other organizations being so close to each other in terms of distance. ### How Is The City Of Mysore?

I feel Mysore is less crowdy than Hyderabad. And its history is fascinating, Mysore being ruled by multiple empires including Cholas, Mughals, and the British before India became independent. It is majorly known for its sandalwood, silk, IT operations, and pharma companies.


### How Is State Of Odisha?

Odisha is also a fascinating state being historic with the famous Kalinga War during 261 BCE & its Jagannatha Puri culture where thousands of people gather in crowds for the significant day. Baseline of its temperature is quite stable, with only problem being the humidity which often makes skin oily or sticky.
