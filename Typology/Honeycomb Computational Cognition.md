## 2. Basic Terminologies


### 2.1 <u>Subjective Baseline</u>:

- **Consciousness:** Ability to have a default computational mode.
- **Ego:** Ability to differentiate between subject and object.
- **Content:** Information that comes into attention of consciousness.


### 2.2 <u>Sources Of Information</u>:

- **Subject:** A processing function made by combining consciousness and ego.
- **Object:** Everything other the subject.
- **System:** Alternative term used for *object*.


### 2.3 <u>Primitives Types</u>:

- **Information elements ($S$, $N$, $T$, $F$):** A form of information that the system possesses.
- **Attitude ($I$, $E$):** Mode of processing information that the system possesses.



## 3. Constraints On Primitives


### 3.1 <u>List Of Primitives</u>:

- Introversion ($I$)
- Extroversion ($E$)
- Sensing ($S$)
- Intuition ($N$)
- Thinking ($T$)
- Feeling ($F$)


### 3.2 <u>Axioms To Satisfy</u>:

1. Its all about default information computation mode.
2. Its not about end goal, or ability to perform preferred action.
3. It can exist without prior experience, or any kind of resources.
4. It can exist under any form of immediate surrounding or condition.
5. Level of intelligence doesn't affect which mode is there by default.
6. One primitive can not reproduce another in any form.



## 4. Attitude


### 4.1 <u>Introversion (I)</u>:

A mode of processing information in which the subject *decomposes* system with information into sub-systems, or dwells on a certain stage of decomposition graph, keeping the net direction of travelling as *semi-negative*. It can keep *decomposing* each sub-system into smaller sub-systems.


### 4.2 <u>Extroversion (E)</u>:

A mode of processing information in which the subject *composes* larger system using information of the initial system, or dwells on a certain stage of decomposition graph, keeping the net direction of travelling as *semi-positive*. It can keep *composing* each system into larger system.



## 5. Information Types


### 5.1 <u>Impressionistic Information (S/N)</u>:

A category of information types which is *impressionistic* in nature, dealing with immediate *impression* that a system triggers in mind of the subject.


### 5.2 <u>Evaluative Information (T/F)</u>:

A category of information types which is *evaluative* in nature, dealing with *values* that a system possesses, whether concretely or in abstraction.



## 6. Information Elements


### 6.1 <u>Sensing (S)</u>:

A form of information which contains the *sensory aspects* of the system under consideration of subject. These information are directly perceived by the senses.


### 6.2 <u>Intuition (N)</u>:

A form of information which contains the *latent aspects* of the system under consideration of subject. These information are indirect and generally hidden.


### 6.3 <u>Thinking (T)</u>:

A form of information which contains the *factual aspects* of the system under consideration of subject. These information are based on laws and logic.


### 6.4 <u>Feeling (F)</u>:

A form of information which contains the *affinitive aspects* of the system under consideration of subject. These information are based on personal or group preferences.



## 7. Functions


### 7.1 <u>Introverted Sensing (Si)</u>:

Combines attitude of *introversion* and information element of *sensing*. This function is used to decompose the *sensory aspects* of a system into sub-systems containing more *sensory information*, and those sub-system can be broken down further for indefinite number of times.
